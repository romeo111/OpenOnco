# Code of conduct

OpenOnco welcomes respectful collaboration from clinicians, researchers, developers, translators and people preparing questions for their care team.

Use considerate language, discuss evidence rather than attacking people, and make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unsolicited promotion and disclosure of private or patient information are unacceptable.

These expectations apply to issues, PRs and discussions. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close off-topic conversations or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and protect affected people.

For a public conduct problem, use GitHub's report-content controls or a minimal issue requesting maintainer attention. Do not repeat private information or abuse. No dedicated private conduct email is currently published. Security vulnerabilities use the separate [reporting process](SECURITY.md).
