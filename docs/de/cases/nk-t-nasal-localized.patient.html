P-GEMOX (pegaspargase + gemcitabine + oxaliplatin) for NK/T-cell nasal lymphoma

Dauer (Text aus der Originalquelle): 4-6 cycles induction; concurrent RT 50 Gy for localized stage I/II; cycle — 21 days

High burden (an intensive regimen)
