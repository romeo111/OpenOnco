RET fusion in papillary thyroid carcinoma (PTC) — ~5-10% sporadic, enriched in radiation-induced PTC. Selpercatinib (LIBRETTO-001 PTC cohort, Wirth 2020 — ORR 79%) and pralsetinib (ARROW PTC cohort, ORR 89%) are FDA-approved for RAI-refractory advanced disease. Selective RET-TKIs are preferred over sorafenib / lenvatinib for RET-fusion-positive RAI-refractory disease.Selpercatinib-Monotherapie (RAI-refraktäre fortgeschrittene Erkrankung)
