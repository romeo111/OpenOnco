Was festgestellt wurde

Dieser Bericht enthält keine Interpretation molekularer Zielstrukturen im Hinblick auf die Behandlung. Was dies für die Behandlung bedeutet

Dieser Status beschreibt Prüfungen des Inhalts der Wissensbasis, keine Freigabe eines Plans für Sie.

A) Hauptoption in diesem Bericht

Isatuximab + Carfilzomib + Dexamethasone (Isa-Kd), 28-day cycles

Dauer (Text aus der Originalquelle): Until progression or unacceptable toxicity; cycle — 28 days

Expected tolerability class:

High burden (an intensive regimen)

This is an intensive regimen that usually requires more frequent monitoring and support. This is an approximate class for the whole regimen, not a prediction for you personally: tolerability depends on your health, doses and supportive treatment.

Prüfung des klinischen Inhalts ausstehend

Isatuximab

an antibody targeting myeloma cells (daratumumab, isatuximab). Erläuterung aus der Originalquelle; Übersetzung nicht verfügbar: Препарат-мітка для клітин мієломи (через CD38, як і daratumumab). Допомагає імунній системі їх знищити. Вводиться внутрішньовенно у складі багатокомпонентних схем.

Medicine form: intravenous infusion (a drip) at the clinic.

Not registered in Ukraine

Carfilzomib

blocks cellular waste disposal; a treatment foundation for multiple myeloma (bortezomib, carfilzomib)

Medicine form: intravenous infusion (a drip) at the clinic.

NHSU covers this medicine, but not for this diagnosis

Dexamethasone

medicine for your condition — discuss the details with your doctor

Medicine form: intravenous infusion (a drip) at the clinic.

Free under the Ukrainian NHSU programme

B) Alternative Option in diesem Bericht

Teclistamab BCMA × CD3 bispezifisch (mit Step-up-Dosierung) — kontinuierlicher SC wöchentlich

Dauer (Text aus der Originalquelle): Continuous until progression or unacceptable toxicity; cycle — 7 days

Expected tolerability class:

High burden (an intensive regimen)

This is an intensive regimen that usually requires more frequent monitoring and support. This is an approximate class for the whole regimen, not a prediction for you personally: tolerability depends on your health, doses and supportive treatment.

Prüfung des klinischen Inhalts ausstehend

Teclistamab

an antibody with two different targets; some such medicines bring T cells close to cancer cells

Medicine form: injection under the skin.

Not registered in Ukraine

Signals requiring immediate attention

No urgent signals were recorded as triggered in this report's trace. This does not rule out emergencies and is not an assessment of your current symptoms.

What to ask your doctor

  • Should I get a second opinion from a molecular oncologist before starting treatment?
  • How many treatment cycles will I need? How will we know whether treatment is working?
  • Which side effects are most likely for me? Which ones can I prevent?
  • Are there support groups or a psychologist for people with cancer that I could see?
  • Is this medicine available in my region? If not, how can I obtain it?
  • Why do you recommend this plan rather than the alternative?
