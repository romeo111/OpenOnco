A) Hauptoption in diesem Bericht

Bortezomib + Rituximab + Dexamethasone (BDR) × 6-8 Zyklen — chemofreie Alternative für r/r WM (einschl. CXCR4-WHIM-mutiert)

Dauer (Text aus der Originalquelle): 6-8 cycles induction; rituximab maintenance optional based on response/depth; cycle — 35 days

Expected tolerability class:

Moderate burden

This regimen can noticeably affect how you feel and requires planned monitoring. This is an approximate class for the whole regimen, not a prediction for you personally: tolerability depends on your health, doses and supportive treatment.
