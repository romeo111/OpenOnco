Zusammenfassung der BeweiseClass 2 BRAF (e.g., K601E, L597, fusions) — RAS-independent, signal as dimers. Class 3 BRAF (e.g., G466, N581, D594) — kinase-impaired, RAS-dependent. NCI-MATCH subprotocol H (trametinib for non-V600 BRAF) showed limited responses.
