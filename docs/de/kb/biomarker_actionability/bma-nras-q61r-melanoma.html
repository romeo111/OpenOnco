Zusammenfassung der BeweiseNRAS Q61R is the most common NRAS hotspot in melanoma (~20% of cutaneous melanomas). Binimetinib (MEKi) monotherapy improved PFS vs dacarbazine in NEMO (Dummer et al. Lancet Oncol 2017) — modest benefit; not FDA-approved as monotherapy. ICI (anti-PD1 ± anti-CTLA4) is the standard 1L for NRAS-mut metastatic melanoma. MEKi+CDK4/6i combos under investigation.
