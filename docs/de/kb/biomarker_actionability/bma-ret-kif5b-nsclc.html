Zusammenfassung der BeweiseKIF5B-RET is the most common RET fusion in NSCLC (~70% of RET+ NSCLC). Treatment is identical to gene-level RET fusion: selpercatinib (LIBRETTO-001 / LIBRETTO-431) and pralsetinib (ARROW). Deep CNS penetrance with selpercatinib (intracranial ORR 82%).
