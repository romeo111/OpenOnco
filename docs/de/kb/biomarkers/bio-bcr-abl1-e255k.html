Anmerkungen

Classical P-loop mutation of ABL1. Reduces nilotinib sensitivity (IC50 ~5-7x baseline) and imatinib sensitivity (IC50 ~10-20x); associated with adverse prognosis on imatinib. Dasatinib, bosutinib, ponatinib retain in-vitro activity. Per ELN-2020 / ELN-2025: detection on nilotinib or imatinib failure → switch to dasatinib (preferred 2G-TKI option) or ponatinib. P-loop mutations as a class (E255K, Y253H, G250E) generally favor dasatinib-based salvage; T315I excluded per separate biomarker.
