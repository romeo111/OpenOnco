Anmerkungen

Cross-disease relevance — CD5+ B-cell lineage marker: - **CLL/SLL** (defining): CD5+ CD23+ classic immunophenotype - **MCL** (defining): CD5+ CD23− cyclin-D1+ — distinguishes from CLL - **DLBCL**: CD5+ DLBCL is adverse subset (~5-10%) — worse R-CHOP outcomes - **Burkitt** typically CD5− - **FL** typically CD5− - **MZL** typically CD5− - **PTCL** also CD5+ (T-cell marker; differential context) Used primarily for diagnostic differentiation rather than algorithm routing. CD5+ DLBCL future scope: candidate for early CAR-T pathway consideration.
