Anmerkungen

5-variable score: each adverse factor = 1 point. Risk groups (R-IPI): 0 = very good (5y OS ~94%); 1-2 = good (~80%); 3-5 = poor (~55%). NCCN-IPI refines age + LDH cuts; gives finer stratification. POLARIX trial used IPI ≥2 to select Pola-R-CHP over R-CHOP — primary determinant in our ALGO-DLBCL-1L decision tree.
