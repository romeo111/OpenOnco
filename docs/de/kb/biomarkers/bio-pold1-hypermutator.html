Anmerkungen

Less frequent than POLE-EDM but mechanistically equivalent. Grouped with POLE in TCGA/TransPORTEC integrated endometrial classification. Triggers the same Stage I de-escalation logic (RF-POLE-POLD1-ENDOMETRIAL-LOW-RISK + IND-ENDOMETRIAL-STAGE-I-POLE-OBSERVATION). Germline variants implicate PPAP — refer to genetic counseling.
