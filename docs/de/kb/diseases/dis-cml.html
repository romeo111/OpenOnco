Anmerkungen

Etiologically-driven archetype (single causal driver: BCR-ABL1). TKI choice in chronic phase 1L is the central question, driven by ELTS risk + comorbidity matrix: imatinib for low-risk / elderly / cost-constrained; nilotinib or dasatinib or bosutinib for higher-risk young targeting deep molecular response + treatment-free remission; ponatinib / asciminib reserved for T315I or ≥3L. Response milestones by IS BCR-ABL1: ≤10% at 3 mo, ≤1% at 6 mo, ≤0.1% (MMR) at 12 mo. STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff before publication. Myeloid expansion (heme).
