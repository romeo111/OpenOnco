Anmerkungen

Two biological subgroups (HPV-driven vs HPV-independent) managed similarly anatomically. Treatment: early-stage (Tis, Ta, T1a) — organ-sparing approaches (topical 5-FU / imiquimod for Tis; CO2- laser or wide local excision for T1a; glansectomy for selected T2); advanced primary (T2-T4) — partial / total penectomy; inguinal nodal management — dynamic sentinel node biopsy or modified inguinal lymphadenectomy per cN0 vs cN+ status (highly morbid, complications common); metastatic — cisplatin + 5-FU OR TIP (paclitaxel + ifosfamide + cisplatin) per EAU; pembrolizumab in MSI-H subset. HPV vaccination of boys (Gardasil-9) is the primary prevention modality alongside neonatal circumcision discussion.
