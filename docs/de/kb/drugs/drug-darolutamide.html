Drug Facts

KlasseAndrogenrezeptor-Rezeptor-Inhibitor (ARPI)
VerfahrenAR antagonist with minimal CNS penetration (low blood-brain-barrier crossing) — favorable cognitive / fall profile vs enzalutamide. Approved for nmCRPC (ARAMIS) and mHSPC (ARASENS, in combination with docetaxel).

Anmerkungen

Best safety profile of the ARPIs (no excess seizures, falls, cognitive AEs). Preferred for nmCRPC in elderly. ARASENS triplet (ADT + darolutamide + docetaxel) standard for high-volume mHSPC where infrastructure permits.
