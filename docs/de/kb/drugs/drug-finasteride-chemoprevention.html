Drug Facts

Klasse5-Alpha-Reduktase-Typ-2-Inhibitor
VerfahrenCompetitive Hemmkörper der 5-alpha-Reduktase Typ 2 Isoenzym; blockiert die Umwandlung von Testosteron zu Dihydrotestosterone (DHT) in Prostata, Haarfollikel und Haut. Reduziert intraprostatische DHT um ~85-90%, während Serumtestotestoester ~10-15% steigt. Prostatakrebs Prävention Begründung: Senkung DHT reduziert androgen-getriebene epitheliale Proliferation. Prostatakrebspräventionsstudie (PCPT, NCT00000484, Thompson NEJM 2003 + 2013 Langzeit): 25% relative Reduktion der Inzidenz von Prostatakrebs nach 7 Jahren, aber Signal für einen erhöhten Gleason ≥7 Nachweis (re-analyziert und teilweise auf Detektionsneigung zurückzuführen; Langzeitmortalität nicht erhöht).
Typische DosierungProstate cancer chemoprevention (controversial / off-label, PCPT protocol): 5 mg PO once daily ongoing. BPH dosing identical (5 mg/day). Time to plateau effect on intraprostatic DHT ~6 months; PSA values reduce by ~50% within 6-12 months — double measured PSA for cancer-screening interpretation in users.

Anmerkungen

STUB — v0.2 chemoprevention-workstream authoring; pending two-Clinical- Co-Lead signoff per CHARTER §6.1 dev-mode. CONTROVERSIAL chemoprevention indication. PCPT (Thompson NEJM 2003): 25% reduction in overall prostate cancer at 7 years BUT initially-reported increase in high-grade (Gleason 7-10) tumors. Subsequent reanalysis attributed much of the high-grade excess to detection bias (smaller prostate volume → easier biopsy detection); long-term mortality at 18 years not increased. AUA 2011 / USPSTF 2018 caution: shared decision-making required; no general- population recommendation. Strict pregnancy exclusion for partner; pregnant women must not handle. Engine should NOT auto-recommend; reserved for high-risk men opting in after explicit counseling.
