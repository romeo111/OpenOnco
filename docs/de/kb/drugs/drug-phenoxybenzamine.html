Drug Facts

KlasseNichtselektiver, irreversibler Alpha-1/alpha-2 adrenerge Rezeptorantagonist
VerfahrenCovalently and irreversibly binds alpha-1 and alpha-2 adrenergic receptors, producing a long-acting, non-competitive blockade of catecholamine-mediated vasoconstriction. In pheochromocytoma / paraganglioma (PPGL), preoperative alpha-blockade with phenoxybenzamine (or a selective alpha-1 blocker such as doxazosin) is mandatory before surgery to prevent an intraoperative hypertensive crisis from catecholamine release during tumor manipulation (Endocrine Society 2014 PPGL guideline). Alpha-2 blockade component can cause reflex tachycardia and orthostatic hypotension, which is more pronounced than with selective alpha-1 agents.
Typische DosierungStarting dose typically 10 mg PO BID, titrated upward in 10-20 mg/day increments every few days to achieve blood-pressure normalization and resolution of orthostatic/paroxysmal symptoms, per Endocrine Society 2014 PPGL guideline (SRC-ENDOCRINE-SOCIETY-PPGL-2014); usual preoperative course runs roughly one to two weeks before surgery to allow adequate, sustained alpha-blockade and intravascular volume re-expansion. Exact target BP/HR thresholds and final maintenance dose are individualized by the treating team and are NOT fixed numeric values in this stub -- see RF-PHEO-INADEQUATE-ALPHA-BLOCKADE for the hard sequencing gate this drug exists to satisfy.

Anmerkungen

Historisch gesehen ist der Richtlinie-bevorzugte Wirkstoff für präoperative PPGL-Alphablockade, da seine irreversible, langwirksame Blockade bei der Tumorbehandlung mit einem Catecholamin-Anstieg schwieriger zu überwinden ist; Doxazosin (selektiver, reversibler Alpha-1-Blocker; siehe DRUG-DOXAZOSIN) ist eine weit verbreitete, verfügbare und besser verträgliche Alternative mit weniger Reflextachykardie -- die Wahl zwischen den beiden ist pro lokaler Praxis/Verfügbarkeit, nicht als KB-Level-Vorliebe modelliert.
