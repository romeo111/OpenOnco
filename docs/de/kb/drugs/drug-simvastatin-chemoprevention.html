Drug Facts

KlasseHMG-CoA-Reduktase-Inhibitor (Statin) — lipophil, erste Generation
VerfahrenLipophiles Prodrug Statin; intrazellulär hydrolysiert in die aktive Beta-Hydroxy-Säure-Form, die wettbewerbsfähig HMG-CoA-Reduktase hemmt. Krebs-Präventions-Rational: gemeinsame pleiotrope Statin-Effekte (verminderte Isoprenoid-Prenylierung von Ras/Rho, entzündungshemmend, antiproliferativ, pro-apoptotisch) plus das ursprüngliche 4S-Secondary-CV-Präventiv RCT Beobachtungssignal (niedrigere Kolorektal-Krebs-Inzidenz). Höheres Rhabdomyolyserisiko als Atorvastatin/Rosuvastatin bei hohen Dosen; FDA-Dosislimit 80 mg aufgrund des Myopathiesignals im Jahr 2011 eingeschränkt.
Typische DosierungCardiovascular-prevention dosing range: 5-40 mg PO once daily (evening dosing recommended due to peak nocturnal cholesterol synthesis). Typical starting dose 10-20 mg/day; titrate per LDL target. 80 mg restricted by FDA (2011) — limit to patients who have tolerated 80 mg ≥12 months without myopathy. No specific oncology-prevention dose. Hepatic monitoring (baseline LFTs); CK only if symptomatic. CrCl <30: start at 5 mg.

Anmerkungen

STUB — v0.2 chemoprevention-workstream authoring (batch 3); pending two- Clinical-Co-Lead signoff per CHARTER §6.1 dev-mode. STATIN CLASS-DETAIL entity (alongside DRUG-ATORVASTATIN-CHEMOPREVENTION and DRUG- ROSUVASTATIN-CHEMOPREVENTION). Primary clinical use remains cardiovascular. Cancer-incidence signals from observational meta- analyses are modest (~5-15% relative reductions in select sites) and have NOT translated to RCT-level cancer-prevention recommendations from USPSTF, NCCN, or ESMO. Engine should NOT recommend statin initiation for cancer prevention; appropriate when CV indications justify use. Heavy CYP3A4-substrate profile — substantial contraindicated-combination list — makes simvastatin a riskier choice than atorvastatin/rosuvastatin in patients on polypharmacy.
