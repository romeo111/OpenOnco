Ursprung der Roten Flagge

BegriffsbestimmungConfirmed germline pathogenic / likely-pathogenic variant in ANKRD26 (Thrombocytopenia 2, THC2; OMIM 188000). Most pathogenic variants cluster in the 5′-untranslated region (the ANKRD26 5′UTR hotspot, chr10:27,389-27,420), disrupting RUNX1 / FLI1-mediated repression and producing a persistent ANKRD26 over-expression phenotype during megakaryopoiesis. Patient has had germline panel testing returned positive, ideally on buccal / fibroblast germline tissue. The carrier presents from birth / childhood with mild-to-moderate isolated thrombocytopenia (platelet count 40-120 x10⁹/L typically) plus a variably-penetrant bleeding diathesis (mild mucocutaneous bleeding, surgical bleeding risk).
