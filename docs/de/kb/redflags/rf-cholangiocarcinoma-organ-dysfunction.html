Anmerkungen

Child-Pugh B/C: cisplatin component should be omitted or replaced; consider gemcitabine monotherapy or capecitabine. FGFR2-inhibitors (pemigatinib, futibatinib, infigratinib) require dose adjustment in hepatic impairment per FDA labels. Ivosidenib (IDH1) requires QTc monitoring + dose reduction with hepatic dysfunction. CrCl <30: cisplatin contraindicated — substitute carboplatin or omit. ABC-02 / TOPAZ-1 / KEYNOTE-966 enrolled mostly preserved hepatic function; extrapolation to Child-Pugh B requires individualized risk-benefit.
