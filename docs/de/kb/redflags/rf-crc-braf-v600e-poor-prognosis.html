Anmerkungen

~30% of BRAF-mutant CRC are MSI-H — RF-CRC-MSI-H-ACTIONABILITY takes precedence (immunotherapy-track outranks chemo-intensification). In MSS BRAF-V600E mutants (the harder subgroup), TRIBE-2 / FOLFOXIRI+bev has ORR 56% but inferior to immunotherapy combinations under study.
