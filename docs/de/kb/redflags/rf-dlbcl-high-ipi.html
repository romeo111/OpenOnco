Anmerkungen

Per POLARIX trial: in IPI ≥2 newly-diagnosed DLBCL, Pola-R-CHP showed superior PFS (~6.5% absolute at 2 years) vs R-CHOP. Toxicity profile comparable except higher peripheral neuropathy with polatuzumab. Major access constraint in Ukraine — polatuzumab not НСЗУ-reimbursed; flag funding pathway in Plan.
