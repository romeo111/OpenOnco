Ursprung der Roten Flagge

BegriffsbestimmungChronic residential ambient air-pollution exposure with PM2.5 annual average >35 µg/m³ (WHO 2021 Interim Target 1; WHO Guideline target 5 µg/m³ annual mean is the long-term benchmark). Outdoor air pollution (specifically PM2.5 + diesel exhaust components) is an IARC Group 1 carcinogen (Monograph 109, 2013; reaffirmed Monograph 133 process) — established etiologic driver of lung cancer (both NSCLC adenocarcinoma + squamous-cell), with population-attributable fraction estimated 3-5% globally + higher in high-exposure regions. Meta-analyses (Hamra 2014; Pope 2020): each 10 µg/m³ increment in PM2.5 raises lung-cancer incidence by ~9% (RR ~1.09). Mechanisms: carbonaceous particle deposition in alveolar / small-airway epithelium; oxidative + inflammatory injury; transition-metal + PAH content as direct mutagens; KRAS/EGFR mutation enrichment in PM2.5-exposed non-smoker adenocarcinoma cohorts...
