Ursprung der Roten Flagge

BegriffsbestimmungLong-term thiopurine exposure ≥3-5 years — azathioprine or its active metabolite 6-mercaptopurine (6-MP) — across inflammatory bowel disease (Crohn disease, ulcerative colitis), rheumatoid arthritis, systemic lupus erythematosus, autoimmune hepatitis, transplant maintenance, and other autoimmune indications. IARC Group 1 carcinogen (azathioprine). Mechanism: thio-purine metabolites incorporate into DNA → impaired mismatch-repair + photo-sensitization (UV-A absorption by 6-thioguanine in DNA generates reactive oxygen species). Anmerkungen

Prevention-persona RedFlag for long-term ≥3y thiopurine (azathioprine / 6-MP) exposure. Engine routes to PreventionPlan recommending (1) annual whole-body skin exam by dermatologist + aggressive sun-protection counseling, (2) EBV serology baseline check in young adults pre-initiation (avoid initiation in EBV-seronegative young men per ECCO 2019), (3) annual B-symptom + lymphadenopathy review, (4) shared-decision discussion of indication continuation vs alternatives (MTX for RA, biologics for IBD) at year 5, (5) HPV vaccination catch-up where applicable. Anchored to DIS-DLBCL-NOS (lymphoma) + DIS-BCC (closest skin-cancer anchor for SCC and BCC). STUB pending two-Clinical-Co-Lead signoff per CHARTER §6.1 dev-mode.
