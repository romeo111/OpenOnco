Ursprung der Roten Flagge

BegriffsbestimmungCurrent or prior tamoxifen exposure ≥5 years in a woman with intact uterus (no prior hysterectomy). Tamoxifen is a selective estrogen- receptor modulator (SERM) — antagonist in breast tissue (therapeutic for ER+ breast cancer + risk reduction) but partial agonist in endometrium → ~2-7 fold increased endometrial cancer risk (predominantly endometrioid; less commonly serous, carcinosarcoma). Cumulative risk ~1-2% over 5y treatment; higher with longer duration + post-menopausal status. Endometrial cancers in tamoxifen-exposed women often more aggressive histology than spontaneous. Population with hysterectomy: this RF does NOT fire (no at-risk organ). Patient with ER+ breast cancer (treatment-time persona) ALSO has prevention- persona considerations covered by this RF. Prevention-persona RedFlag (§20 v0.3 iatrogenic pilot).

Anmerkungen

Präventivpersona RedFlag für Tamoxifen-Exposition ≥5y bei Frauen mit intakter Gebärmutter. ~2-7-fachen endometrisches Krebsrisiko. Motorrouten zur PräventionPlan empfehlen symptom-prompte Auswertung (postmenopausale Blutung → sofortiges Aufarbeiten mit TVUS + endemetrische Biopsie). Routine-asymptomatisches TVUS-Screening NICHT empfohlen per ACOG (false-positive aus Tamoxipen-bedingten subepithelialen Veränderungen).
