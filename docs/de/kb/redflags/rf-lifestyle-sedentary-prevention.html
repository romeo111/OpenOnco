Ursprung der Roten Flagge

BegriffsbestimmungLow physical activity / sedentary lifestyle — defined as <150 min/week moderate-intensity OR <75 min/week vigorous-intensity aerobic activity per WHO + US DHHS Physical Activity Guidelines, OR cumulative sedentary time ≥8h/day. Inverse association with multiple cancers — strongest evidence for colorectal cancer (~20-25% risk reduction with adequate activity per WCRF/AICR convincing-evidence rating), endometrial cancer (~20%), postmenopausal breast cancer (~10-15%). Probable additional protection for esophageal adenocarcinoma, kidney, bladder, gastric- cardia, lung. Mechanisms: insulin/IGF-1 axis, sex hormones, chronic inflammation, body fatness mediation. Prevention-persona RedFlag (§20 v0.3 lifestyle pilot).
