Anmerkungen

AdvSM (ASM, SM-AHN, mast cell leukemia) patients are typically older (median ~65). Per NCCN-SM v.X.2025: midostaurin or avapritinib preferred for advSM but require careful monitoring (QT, hepatic); frailty steers toward supportive management + symptomatic mediator- blockade (H1/H2 antihistamines, cromolyn, omalizumab) over cytoreduction. STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff.
