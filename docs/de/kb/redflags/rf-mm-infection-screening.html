Anmerkungen

Daratumumab + dexamethasone amplifies HBV reactivation risk vs bortezomib-only regimens — entecavir or tenofovir prophylaxis from cycle-1 day-1 through 12 months post-last-dara. HIV+ MM uses HIV-aware regimen choice (no R, no rituximab — same VRd works on ART); baseline CD4 documentation required. Active TB: complete intensive phase before steroids + anti-CD38.
