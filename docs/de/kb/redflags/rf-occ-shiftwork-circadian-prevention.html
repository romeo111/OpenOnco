Anmerkungen

Prevention-persona RedFlag — v0.3 IARC 2A probable-carcinogen batch (2026-05-19). IARC Group 2A probable — limited human evidence; clinical recommendation more conservative than Group 1. IARC Monograph 124 (2020) is the canonical evidence anchor and is NOT yet a separate SRC entity in KB; cited in notes only. Anchor scope: breast cancer ONLY. IARC 124 classifies "limited evidence in humans" specifically for breast cancer; other cancers (prostate, colorectal) carry "inadequate evidence" and are deliberately excluded from relevant_diseases. Female breast is the dominant endpoint; male breast cancer in night-shift cohorts is insufficiently powered for meaningful inference.
