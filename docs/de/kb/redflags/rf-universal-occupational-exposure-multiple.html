Ursprung der Roten Flagge

BegriffsbestimmungPatient with documented ≥2 distinct IARC Group 1 occupational carcinogen exposures during working life. Examples of synergistic paired exposures: asbestos + tobacco smoking (lung-cancer relative risk ~50-90 in combined cohort vs ~5 for tobacco alone, ~5 for asbestos alone — supra-multiplicative interaction at low asbestos doses); benzene + radon; polycyclic-aromatic-hydrocarbon (PAH) exposure + tobacco; diesel-exhaust + silica; formaldehyde + wood- dust in joinery / cabinetmaking; ionizing-radiation + tobacco (lung carcinogenesis); painter / coke-oven / aluminum-smelter worker + tobacco. IARC Monographs Volumes 100A-F + 105 (diesel) + 132 (firefighting) document the individual Group 1 status and the WHO REACH database documents the synergistic combinations. Universal RedFlag (relevant_diseases ["*"]) — fires only when the occupational-history questionnaire documents ≥2 distinct Group 1...
