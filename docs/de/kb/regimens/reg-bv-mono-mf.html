Anmerkungen

ALCANZA trial (Prince 2017, Lancet): brentuximab vedotin monotherapy vs methotrexate or bexarotene in CD30+ CTCL — superior ORR4 (66.7% vs 16.7%). NCCN cat 1 for CD30+ MF (≥10% by IHC) and primary cutaneous ALCL. Different from CHP-Bv (combination chemo backbone) — this is single-agent for cutaneous-predominant disease without systemic chemo intensity.
