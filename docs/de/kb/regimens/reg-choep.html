Anmerkungen

Used for fit younger PTCL NOS / AITL when CD30 negative or brentuximab not accessible. CHOEP (with etoposide) showed improvement over CHOP in younger T-cell patients per Nordic data. 6 cycles + autoSCT consolidation in fit transplant-eligible — practice standard for PTCL/AITL despite limited RCT evidence.

CHOEP21 monitoring follows its own 21-day regimen and drug-specific toxicity assessment. It must not inherit CD20-positive DLBCL, double-hit, IPI, CNS-IPI or anti-CD20-specific checkpoints from R-CHOP. Neuropathy assessment concerns vincristine; etoposide administrations occur on days 1-3. Count-based delay and growth-factor decisions require CHOEP-specific clinical review.
