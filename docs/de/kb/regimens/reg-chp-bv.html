Anmerkungen

ECHELON-2-Studie (Horwitz 2019): CHP-Bv für CD30+ systemische ALCL + CD30 + PTCL/AITL überlegen — verbesserte PFS + OS (~85% 3y PFS für ALCL ALK-). Vincristine REPLACED durch Brentuximab (Vermeidung additiver MMAE-Neuropathie-Stack). Patienten müssen CD30 über ≥10% durch IHC. ALCL ist universell CD30 plus → CHP Bv bevorzugt. PTCL NOS / AITL ~40-60% CD30 Plus → CHK-BV, wenn Ausdruck bestätigt; sonst CHOEP.

SRC-EVIQ-BV-CHP-4038

eviQ 4038 Treatment schedule, Clinical information, Dose modifications/Haematological toxicity and Pre-treatment assessment.

For adults with previously untreated PTCL receiving ADCETRIS plus CHP, the US label specifies primary G-CSF prophylaxis beginning with Cycle 1. eviQ 4038 instead describes use at clinician discretion and secondary support after ANC-related delay. Check applicable product information and local policy; fever or suspected infection remains urgent despite prophylaxis.

Primary G-CSF with adult previously untreated PTCL/BV-CHP only; not monotherapy, all brentuximab regimens or individualized administration timing.
