Anmerkungen

MPACT trial: gem-nab-pac vs gemcitabine in metastatic PDAC PS 0-2 — mOS 8.5 vs 6.7 mo, ORR 23 vs 7%. Better-tolerated alternative to FOLFIRINOX for ECOG 1-2 / older / borderline-fit patients. Less cumulative neuropathy + neutropenia than FOLFIRINOX but more day-15 cytopenia.
