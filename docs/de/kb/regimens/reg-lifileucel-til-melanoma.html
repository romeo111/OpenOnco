Anmerkungen

C-144-01 pooled cohorts (n=153): ORR 31.4%, mDoR not reached at 27.6 mo follow-up, mOS 13.9 mo — first cellular therapy approved for any solid tumor (FDA 2024-02-16, Iovance Amtagvi). Single- infusion treatment intent (no maintenance). Treatment-related mortality ~7% — patient selection critical: ECOG ≤1, organ function adequate for IL-2 (LVEF ≥45, FEV1 ≥60, DLCO ≥60), no active infection, no chronic immunosuppression. CRITICAL: must AVOID systemic corticosteroids before and shortly after TIL infusion (abrogate TIL function). Specialty-center authorization + REMS-style accreditation required. Ukraine pathway: international referral only.
