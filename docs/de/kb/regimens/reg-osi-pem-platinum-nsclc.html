Anmerkungen

FLAURA-2 (Planchard NEJM 2023, NCT04035486): osimertinib 80 mg PO daily + pemetrexed 500 mg/m² IV q3w + carboplatin AUC 5 OR cisplatin 75 mg/m² IV q3w × 4 cycles, then osi + pemetrexed maintenance until progression. Median PFS by investigator 25.5 vs 16.7 mo (HR 0.62; p<0.001) vs osi monotherapy; BICR PFS 29.4 vs 19.9 mo. CNS PFS HR 0.40 in patients with baseline CNS metastases. Higher Grade ≥3 AE rate (64% vs 27%) — additive cytopenia, mucositis, fatigue. Reserve for high-burden disease (CNS mets, L858R, large tumor volume) where PFS extension justifies added toxicity. FDA approval Feb 2024; NCCN cat 1 alternative-preferred 1L.
