Anmerkungen

KEYNOTE-048 (Burtness Lancet 2019): in PD-L1 CPS ≥1 R/M HNSCC, pembrolizumab + 5-FU + platinum vs EXTREME (cetuximab + 5-FU + platinum) improved OS 13.6 vs 10.4 mo (HR 0.65). In CPS <1 the benefit is smaller; in CPS ≥20 monotherapy pembrolizumab is preferred (less chemo-toxicity, equivalent OS). Cisplatin preferred over carboplatin when feasible; carboplatin is the practical default in elderly / comorbid / renal-impaired. EXTREME (cetuximab + 5-FU + platinum) remains an alternative for IO-contraindicated patients.
