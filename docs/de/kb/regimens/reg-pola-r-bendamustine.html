Anmerkungen

GO29365 randomized phase 1b/2 (Sehn 2020): in r/r DLBCL ineligible for autoSCT, Pola-BR vs BR alone showed CR 40% vs 18%, mPFS 9.5 vs 3.7 mo, mOS 12.4 vs 4.7 mo. Approved 2L+ for transplant-ineligible r/r DLBCL. Active CD20 + CD79b expression both required (post-CD19/CD20 CAR-T patients — verify CD20 retention by repeat IHC). Bendamustine myelosuppression cumulative — GCSF prophylaxis recommended.
