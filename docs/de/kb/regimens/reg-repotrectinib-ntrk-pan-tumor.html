Anmerkungen

TRIDENT-1 NTRK cohort: TKI-naive ORR 58%, mPFS not reached (≥1 yr follow-up), intracranial activity demonstrated. TKI-pretreated NTRK ORR 50% incl. activity against solvent-front (G595R, G623R) and gatekeeper resistance mutations that compromise larotrectinib / entrectinib. FDA approval 2024-06 covers pan-tumor NTRK-fusion+ scope independent of organ-of-origin. Mechanistic edge: rigid macrocyclic scaffold designed to bypass canonical TRK resistance positions. Lead-in dosing (160 mg QD x 14d → 160 mg BID) critical to manage CNS/vestibular AE — direct BID worsens vestibular toxicity. Pediatric ≥12 yo dosing 160 mg QD lead-in then 160 mg BID (adult scheme) per FDA label.
