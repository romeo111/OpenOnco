HER2-amplified metastatic colorectal cancer (~3-5% in RAS/BRAF-WT population): tucatinib + trastuzumab is FDA-approved 2L+ for HER2- positive RAS-WT mCRC based on MOUNTAINEER (Strickler Lancet Oncol 2023 — ORR 38%, mDOR 12 mo) per SRC-NCCN-COLON-2025. Trastuzumab deruxtecan also has activity (DESTINY-CRC01 Siena Lancet Oncol 2021 — ORR 45% in HER2 IHC 3+ RAS-WT) and tumor-agnostic FDA approval (2024) for HER2-positive solid tumors that have progressed on prior therapy. Older trastuzumab + lapatinib (HERACLES) is referenced as alternative. RAS-mutant tumors should not receive HER2-directed therapy in 2L because RAS-driven resistance is well-documented per SRC-NCCN-COLON-2025.tucatinib + trastuzumab (2L+ HER2+ RAS-WT mCRC por SRC-NCCN-COLON-2025)

Qué NO hacer

Explicit prohibitive rules, each grounded in a regimen / supportive care / contraindication entity
Standard plan (IND-CRC-ADJUVANT-STAGE2-HIGHRISK-FOLFOX)
  • Do NOT offer adjuvant chemo to dMMR/MSI-H stage II — no benefit, possible harm
  • NO retrase el adyuvante más allá de 8 semanas después de la cirugía
  • NO agregue bevacizumab — NSABP C-08 negativo
  • Do NOT continue oxaliplatin past Grade 2 functional neuropathy — switch to 5-FU/LV
  • NO aplique los datos de IDEA CAPOX a FOLFOX a 3 meses (no intercambiable para la reducción de la duración)
Standard plan (IND-CRC-ADJUVANT-STAGE3-FOLFOX)
  • NO retrase el adyuvante más allá de 8 semanas después de la cirugía si se puede evitar, disminuyendo el beneficio después
  • Do NOT continue oxaliplatin past Grade 2 functional neuropathy — switch to 5-FU/LV alone
  • NO agregue bevacizumab en el adyuvante — NSABP C-08 negativo, NO es un estándar
Standard plan (IND-CRC-ADJUVANT-STAGE3-CAPOX)
  • Do NOT use CAPOX if CrCl <30 mL/min — capecitabine renally cleared
  • NO agregue bevacizumab — adjuvante bevazizumabe NSABP C-08 negativo
  • NO aplique IDEA datos de 3 meses desde CAPOX a FOLFOX (FOLFoxi 3 mo era inferior)
  • NO retrase el adyuvante más allá de 8 semanas después de la cirugía
  • Do NOT continue oxaliplatin past Grade 2 functional neuropathy — switch to capecitabine alone
