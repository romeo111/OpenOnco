Atención estándar
  • SRC-NCCN-ALL-2025: Level Category 2A (Apoyo, sensibilidad/respuesta)
Revumenib (menin inhibitor) FDA-approved (Nov 2024) for relapsed/refractory KMT2A-rearranged acute leukemia including B-ALL. AUGMENT-101 combined KMT2A-rearranged cohort (AML + ALL): ORR 23%, CR/CRh 18%. KMT2A rearrangement defines a high-risk B-ALL subset: in infants KMT2A-AFF1 is present in ~80% of infant B-ALL (EFS <30% with standard chemotherapy); in adults KMT2A-rearranged B-ALL is adverse-risk (NCCN/ELN). Revumenib represents a first targeted option for this historically chemotherapy-refractory population. Blinatumomab (CD19 BiTE, FDA-approved for R/R B-ALL) and inotuzumab ozogamicin are also active in KMT2A-rearranged B-ALL regardless of KMT2A status, but revumenib is the first mutation-specific approved agent. AlloHCT in CR remains the standard consolidation for eligible adults.revumenib 163 mg PO bid en monoterapia (ajustado para los inhibidores del CYP3A4 a 95 mg bid)
blinatumomab (independente del estado de KMT2A; CD19-señalado; R/R B-ALL por ensayo TOWER)
inotuzumab ozogamicin (CD22-targeted; R/R B-ALL per INO-VATE trial)

CONTRA-AGGRESSIVE

Hard contraindications to escalation
  • Active or latent HBV without antiviral prophylaxis is an absolute contraindication to starting B-cell-depleting / immunomodulatory monoclonal antibody therapy (anti-CD20, anti-CD30 ADC, anti-CD38). Severe HBV reactivation hepatitis risk including fulminant hepatic failure.CI-HBV-NO-PROPHYLAXIS
  • Pre-treatment LVEF <50% is an absolute contraindication to anthracycline-containing regimens (R-CHOP, Pola-R-CHP, ABVD, BV-AVD, etc.). Cardiotoxicity from doxorubicin is dose-cumulative and often irreversible; starting with already-impaired function risks acute decompensation.CI-LVEF-LOW-FOR-ANTHRACYCLINE

Qué NO hacer

Explicit prohibitive rules, each grounded in a regimen / supportive care / contraindication entity
Plan agresivo (IND-B-ALL-1L-PH-NEG)
  • No se salte la profilaxis del SNC.
  • No descuide el seguimiento de MRD- post-inducción: los pts de Mrd+ reciben blinatumomab.
  • Do not give vincristine intrathecally.
  • No iniciar sin pantalla del VHB + profilaxis de entecavir (rituximab).
  • No descuide la profilaxis del SLT en hiperleucocitosis (WBC > 100K) — hipercalemia fatal.
Plan estándar (IND-B-ALL-1L-PH-POS)
  • No inicie sin BCR basal::ABL1 PCR — aumento cuantitativo de la terapia de la DRM.
  • No se salte la profilaxis del SNC (IT MTX/citarabina + HD-MTX intercalada) - la recaída del SCN es fatal.
  • Do not neglect T315I mutation testing — imatinib/dasatinib are useless; ponatinib needed.
  • Do not give vincristine intrathecally — fatal.
  • No inicie el TKI sin ECG (base basal de QTc) — Los TKIS prolongan el QT.
  • Do not reduce TKI dose without cause — under-dosing → resistance.
