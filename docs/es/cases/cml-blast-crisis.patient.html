Qué significa esto para el tratamiento

A) Standard option

Imatinib (CML chronic phase 1L)

Duration (original source text): Continuous; check BCR-ABL1 IS at 3, 6, 12 months for milestone-based response (≤10%, ≤1%, ≤0.1% respectively); TKI switch on suboptimal / failure

Expected tolerability class:

Lower burden

This is usually one of the regimens with a lower overall treatment burden. This is an approximate class for the whole regimen, not a prediction for you personally: tolerability depends on your health, doses and supportive treatment.

Awaiting review by doctors

Imatinib

Original source explanation; translation unavailable: Таблетка, яка вимикає мутацію BCR-ABL у клітинах хронічного мієлоїдного лейкозу (та KIT/PDGFRA при GIST). Зазвичай добре переноситься, потребує щоденного прийому з їжею і великою кількістю води.

Medicine form: tablets taken by mouth.

Free under the Ukrainian NHSU programme

B) Alternative option

2en la generación TKI (dasatinib OR nilotinib OR bosutinib por matriz de comorbilidad) — CML fase crónica 1L para la búsqueda de alto riesgo / más joven TFR

Duration (original source text): Continuous; check BCR-ABL1 IS at 3, 6, 12 months; TFR attempt after ≥3 years if sustained DMR ≥2 years

Expected tolerability class:

Moderate burden

This regimen can noticeably affect how you feel and requires planned monitoring. This is an approximate class for the whole regimen, not a prediction for you personally: tolerability depends on your health, doses and supportive treatment.

Awaiting review by doctors

Dasatinib

Original source explanation; translation unavailable: Таблетка-TKI 2-го покоління: блокує BCR-ABL ефективніше за imatinib і використовується при стійкості або непереносимості. Може спричиняти випіт у плеврі — повідомляйте про задишку.

What to ask your doctor

  • Should I get a second opinion from a molecular oncologist before starting treatment?
  • How many treatment cycles will I need? How will we know whether treatment is working?
  • Which side effects are most likely for me? Which ones can I prevent?
  • Are there support groups or a psychologist for people with cancer that I could see?
  • How will this treatment affect my fertility? Should I have reproductive cells frozen?
  • Why do you recommend this plan rather than the alternative?
