Notas

The legacy high-risk flag does not establish applicability of the encoded eviQ high-risk/no-proven-CNS schedule. The indication requires burkitt_high_risk=true and cns_involvement=false; missing/conflicting or proven-CNS status prevents materialization. This does not validate risk classification, fitness, HIV-specific ranking or CNS-positive/low-risk protocol choices. A separate eviQ proven-CNS reference is exposed only on explicit variant opt-in plus confirmed matching risk/CNS values; choosing a reference is not complete eligibility or clinical approval.
