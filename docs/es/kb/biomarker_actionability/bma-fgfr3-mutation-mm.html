Resumen de las pruebasIn multiple myeloma, t(4;14) translocation places MMSET/FGFR3 under IgH control; ~30% of t(4;14) cases co-express FGFR3, with rare acquired activating point mutations (Y373C, K650E). Despite preclinical sensitivity, clinical trials of FGFR inhibitors (dovitinib, AZD4547) in MM showed limited efficacy; no FGFR-targeted therapy is approved in MM. Treatment is standard t(4;14) high-risk myeloma protocol (proteasome-inhibitor-based).

Notas

ESCAT X — biological rationale present but no actionable clinical evidence. t(4;14) status defines high-risk MM and influences proteasome-inhibitor-based regimen choice (KRd, IsaKRd, daratumumab combos), but FGFR3 itself is not a treatment target in MM.
