Resumen de las pruebasFLT3-D835 (TKD) in newly-diagnosed AML: midostaurin + 7+3 was studied in FLT3-mutant (ITD or TKD) AML in RATIFY (Stone 2017) — benefit driven primarily by ITD subset, with smaller magnitude in TKD. Quizartinib (type II FLT3i) is INACTIVE against TKD D835 (target alternate conformation). Gilteritinib (type I) and midostaurin retain activity. ELN 2022 prognostic impact of D835 alone is intermediate.

Notas

ESCAT IB. OncoKB Nivel 1. Punto crítico de selección de TKI: D835 confiere resistencia al tipo II FLT3i (quizartinib, sorafenib) pero NO al tipo I FLT 3i (gilteritinib. midostaurin. crenolanib). Diagnóstico de compañero: LeukoStrat CDx detecta tanto la DTI como la DTD D835.
