Resumen de las pruebasNTRK fusion in NSCLC is rare (~0.1-0.5%). Larotrectinib and entrectinib are FDA tumor-agnostic; NSCLC subset of pooled trials shows durable responses including CNS activity (entrectinib has superior CNS penetrance). Tumor-agnostic actionability — NTRK testing is part of comprehensive NGS panels in NSCLC.
