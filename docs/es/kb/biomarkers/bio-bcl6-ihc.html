Notas

BCL6 IHC is a core component of the Hans algorithm for DLBCL cell-of-origin classification. While gene expression profiling (GEP, Lymph2Cx assay) is the reference standard for GCB/ABC classification, Hans IHC is widely used in resource-limited settings (concordance with GEP ~80%). BCL6 protein expression is distinct from BCL6 chromosomal rearrangement (t(3;v) translocations detected by FISH) — both exist independently. BCL6 rearrangement occurs in ~25-35% of DLBCL and may be present in either GCB or non-GCB subtypes.
