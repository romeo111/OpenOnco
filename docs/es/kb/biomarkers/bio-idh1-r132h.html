Notas

Defining feature of IDH-mutant glioma (per WHO 2021 CNS5 — distinct diagnostic class) and ~6-10% of AML. IHC (mIDH1-R132H antibody) is highly sensitive for this specific variant — used as first-pass glioma screen. Other IDH1 variants (R132C, R132G, R132S, R132L) are rarer and IHC-negative — require NGS. Ivosidenib (AGILE, AML 1L combo with azacitidine in IDH1-mut AML), enasidenib (IDH2 R140/R172), vorasidenib (INDIGO 2024 — first-in-class for grade 2 IDH-mut glioma post-resection — practice-changing). 2-HG MRS imaging emerging as noninvasive activity readout.
