Notas

LCT marks aggressive transformation of MF — prognosis worsens substantially (median OS ~2-4 years from LCT diagnosis vs decades for early-stage MF). Often CD30+ (~30-50%) → eligible for brentuximab vedotin per ALCANZA trial. Mandates systemic therapy reassessment regardless of TNMB stage. Distinct from primary cutaneous CD30+ ALCL (different entity, different prognosis).
