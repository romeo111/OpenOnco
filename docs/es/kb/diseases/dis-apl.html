Notas

Separated from DIS-AML because clinical logic is fundamentally different: (a) emergency ATRA initiation on suspicion (within hours of presentation), (b) DIC management is the dominant early task, (c) chemotherapy-free ATRA + ATO cures low-risk patients (APL0406 / LO-CoCo), (d) high-risk uses ATRA + ATO + idarubicin (or classic AIDA). NCCN and ELN both treat APL as a separate chapter — we mirror that. CHARTER §15.2 C2 — emergency initiation is partially time-critical (outside non-device CDS carve-out for the first dose); our scope is workup + plan AFTER initial stabilization. STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff before publication. Myeloid expansion (heme).
