Capacidades

Notas

Functional adenomas classified by lineage / hormone hypersecretion; treatment is endocrine-cohort-specific: prolactinoma — dopamine agonists (cabergoline > bromocriptine) first-line, surgery for resistant / intolerant; somatotroph (acromegaly) — transsphenoidal surgery first-line, somatostatin analogs (octreotide LAR, lanreotide, pasireotide LAR), GH-receptor antagonist (pegvisomant) for residual; corticotroph (Cushing disease) — surgery, pasireotide, ketoconazole / metyrapone / osilodrostat for cortisol control; nonfunctioning — surgery for mass effect / visual compromise. MEN1 carriers: cascade screening from age 5 (prolactin + IGF-1 + serum Ca + intact PTH + fasting gastrin + pituitary MRI per protocol).
