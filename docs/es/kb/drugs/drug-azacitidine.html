Datos sobre drogas

ClaseAgente hipometilante (analógico de citidina)
MecanismoPyrimidine nucleoside analog incorporated into DNA and RNA, inhibits DNA methyltransferases → re-expression of silenced tumor-suppressor genes + cytotoxicity at higher doses. Backbone of MDS-HR, AML-unfit (with venetoclax), and CMML 1L therapy.
Dosificación típicaSC/IV 75 mg/m²/day × 7 days every 28-day cycle (most common); 5-2-2 schedule (5 d on, 2 d off, 2 d on) acceptable. Continue ≥6 cycles before declaring failure (response often delayed 4-6 cycles). Ven+aza for AML: azacitidine 75 mg/m² days 1-7 + venetoclax ramp.
