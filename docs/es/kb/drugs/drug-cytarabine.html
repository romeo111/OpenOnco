MecanismoPhosphorylated intracellularly to ara-CTP, incorporated into DNA, inhibits DNA polymerase, terminates chain elongation. Cell-cycle S-phase specific. Backbone of AML induction; high-dose for consolidation + CNS prophylaxis (crosses BBB at HD).

Advertencias

  • Mielosupresión grave
  • Toxicidad cerebelar en HiDAC (riesgo relacionado con la dosis, edad > 50 años)
  • Conjuntivitis con HiDAC (profilaxis obligatoria de colirio esteroide)

Notas

Used in: AML induction (7+3), AML consolidation (HiDAC), MCL intensive (R-DHAP / R-DHAOx for fit transplant-eligible), Burkitt (CODOX-M/IVAC), CNS prophylaxis IT. HiDAC requires careful elderly dose selection (1.5 g/m² instead of 3 g/m² over 65) + eye drops + neurologic exam pre each dose.

Utilizado por

Esquema terapéutico

  • REG-AAML1031-PEDIATRIC - AAML1031- inducción de estilo (Cytarabina + Daunorubicin + Etoposide, pediátrica AML 1L)

  • REG-AML-7-3 - 7+3 Induction (cytarabine + daunorubicin/idarubicin) ± midostaurin
  • REG-AML-7-3-GO - 7+3 + fractionated gemtuzumab ozogamicin (CD33+ AML, 1L; ALFA-0701)
