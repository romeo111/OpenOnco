Datos sobre drogas

ClaseInhibidor de mTOR (rapalog)
MecanismoAllosteric inhibitor of mTORC1 — blocks PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway downstream of AKT. In HR+/HER2- breast: reverses endocrine resistance when added to AI (BOLERO-2 with exemestane) or fulvestrant (PrECOG/MANTA). Also approved in advanced RCC, pNET, and SEGA in tuberous sclerosis.
Dosificación típica10 mg PO una vez al día continuo + exemestane 25 mg PO diariamente (BOLERO-2) OR + fulvestrant 500 mg IM Calendario estándar (PRECOG 0102).

Notas

Pivotal trial: BOLERO-2 (everolimus + exemestane). Off-label use with fulvestrant supported by PrECOG 0102 (Kornblum 2018) and clinical practice for fulvestrant-pretreated patients. Dexamethasone mouthwash 0.5 mg/5 mL swish/spit BID for first 8 weeks reduces G2+ stomatitis from ~33% to ~2% (SWISH trial).

Utilizado por

Esquema terapéutico

  • REG-EVEROLIMUS-EXEMESTANE - Everolimus + Exemestane (HR+/HER2- pecho 3L+, puesto CDK4/6i y PI3(Ki/AKTi)
  • REG-EVEROLIMUS-MONO-PNET - Everolimus en monoterapia (avanzado/progresista pNET G1/G2; RADIANTE-3)
  • REG-FULVESTRANT-EVEROLIMUS-BREAST - Fulvestrant + everolimus (HR+/HER2- metastásico post-AI/CDK4/6i, sin mutación objetivo)
