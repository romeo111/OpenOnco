ClaseBCR-ABL1 TKI (3la tercera generación, T315I- Activo)
MecanismoPan-BCR-ABL1 TKI active against ALL known kinase-domain mutations including T315I gatekeeper. Reserved for r/r CML/Ph+ ALL after multiple TKI failures or T315I emergence due to substantial vascular toxicity.
Dosificación típicaCML/Ph+ ALL with T315I or post multiple TKI failures: starting 45 mg PO daily; reduce to 15 mg upon achievement of major molecular response (response-adjusted dosing per OPTIC trial reduces vascular events ~50%).

Advertencias

  • Efectos oclusivos arteriales (en caja) — IAM, ictus, PAOD; riesgo de mortalidad

Notas

Reserved for T315I CML or post ≥2 TKI failures. Response-adjusted dosing (45 → 15 mg upon MMR) per OPTIC trial reduces toxicity. Asciminib (STAMP) is an emerging less-toxic alternative for similar indication. NOT registered in Ukraine — major access barrier.
