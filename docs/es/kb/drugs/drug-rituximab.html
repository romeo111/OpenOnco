Datos sobre drogas

Clasemonoclonal_antibody — anti-CD20 chimeric IgG1κ
MecanismoChimeric (mouse-human) monoclonal antibody binding the CD20 antigen on B lymphocytes (pre-B through mature B; sparing pro-B and plasma cells). Depletes CD20+ B cells via three mechanisms: complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC, dominant in CLL), antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC, dominant in lymphoma), and direct apoptosis via CD20 cross-linking. Recovery of normal B cells takes 6-12 months after the last dose.
Dosificación típicaR-CHOP / R-CVP / R-Bendamustine (DLBCL, FL): 375 mg/m² IV day 1 of each 21- or 28-day cycle × 6-8 cycles. R-monotherapy / FL maintenance: 375 mg/m² IV every 8-12 weeks × 2 years (PRIMA / RESORT schedules). CLL (R-FC, R-Ben): 375 mg/m² IV cycle 1 day 1, then 500 mg/m² IV day 1 cycles 2-6. SC formulation (FL, DLBCL): 1400 mg fixed-dose SC over ~5 minutes from cycle 2 onward (after first IV dose for tolerability).

Advertencias

  • Reacciones fatales a la perfusión (en las 24 horas siguientes a la primera dosis; en su mayoría ciclo 1)
  • Reacciones mucocutáneas graves (Stevens-Johnson, TEN, pemfigo paraneoplásico)
  • Reactivación del virus de la hepatitis B: insuficiencia hepática fulminante posible; detección de HBsAg + anti-HBc antes de la primera dosis; profilaxis de entecavir si es positivo
  • Leucoencefalopatía multifocal progresiva (LMP, reactivación del virus JC)

Notas

Foundation anti-CD20 therapy in B-cell lymphomas. Premedication (acetaminophen + diphenhydramine ± methylprednisolone) before each infusion; first-dose infusion rate escalation per protocol (50 mg/h start, doubled q30min to max 400 mg/h). HBV screening (HBsAg + anti-HBc + anti-HBs) is mandatory before first dose; entecavir or tenofovir prophylaxis throughout therapy and ≥12 months post for any HBV-positive serology. SC formulation reduces administration time from 4-6 h to 5-7 minutes and is preferred from cycle 2 onward in FL / DLBCL. For HCV-MZL, reserved for cases not responding to antiviral therapy alone, or aggressive phenotype where immediate cytoreduction is indicated. Biosimilars are clinically interchangeable in UA per НСЗУ guidance.
