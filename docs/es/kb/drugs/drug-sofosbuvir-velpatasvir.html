Notas

Primary DAA regimen for pan-genotypic HCV treatment. SVR12 (cure) rate >95% in non-cirrhotic; 85-95% in decompensated cirrhosis (with ribavirin). Cornerstone of antiviral-first strategy for HCV-MZL.
