Notas

Extrapolation indication — confidence: moderate. DESTINY-Gastric01 + DESTINY-Gastric04 enrolled HER2+ gastric/GEJ adenocarcinoma (Siewert I-III); pure esoph-confined adeno was not formally enrolled. NCCN- Esophageal/EGJ 2025 + ESMO-Esophageal 2024 endorse HER2 testing in metastatic esoph adeno and recommend trastuzumab-based 1L (ToGA backbone); 2L T-DXd is the natural extension of this routing for HER2+ esoph adeno post 1L trastuzumab failure. Reuses REG-TDXD-GASTRIC (gastric T-DXd regimen) unchanged — same dose (6.4 mg/kg q3w), schedule, components for esoph adeno extrapolation. No new regimen authored; reuse audit confirmed identical backbone per DG-04 protocol. Companion to IND-ESOPH-METASTATIC-1L-HER2-TRASTUZUMAB-CHEMO (1L ToGA esoph extrapolation): together they form the HER2+ esoph adeno treatment pathway 1L → 2L matching the gastric pathway.
