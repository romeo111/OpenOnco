Origen de la bandera roja

DefiniciónConfirmed germline pathogenic / likely-pathogenic variant in BRCA1 or BRCA2 — the canonical HBOC (Hereditary Breast and Ovarian Cancer) spectrum genes. Patient has had a germline test returned positive; the family-history-suspicion question is settled. No current personal cancer diagnosis in the carrier being assessed (current cancer diagnosis routes to treatment-track plan with carrier status driving systemic-therapy selection — PARP inhibitor consideration, platinum sensitivity, RRM / RRSO interaction). Prevention-persona RedFlag (§20, v0.2-B confirmed-carrier surveillance pathway — distinct from the family-history-suspicion pathway in RF-BRCA-HBOC-FAMILY-HISTORY-SUSPICION which fires before testing).

Notas

v0.2-B confirmed-carrier surveillance pathway — BRCA1 / BRCA2. Fires on documented germline BRCA pathogenic variant positivity in an asymptomatic individual. Engine routes to PreventionPlan recommending: (a) IND-BRCA-CARRIER-SURVEILLANCE (standard) — NCCN Genetic / Familial High-Risk: Breast, Ovarian, Pancreatic carrier surveillance protocol (annual breast MRI from age 25 alternating with mammogram from 30; clinical breast exam q6mo; TVUS + CA-125 q6mo from age 30-35 framed honestly as suboptimal; PSA + DRE for BRCA2 men from 40) (b) IND-BRCA-CARRIER-INTENSIFIED (aggressive) — standard protocol PLUS discussion of risk-reducing mastectomy (RRM) age 25-30 (BRCA1 > BRCA2 urgency) AND risk-reducing BSO (RRSO) age 35-40 (BRCA1) / 40-45 (BRCA2) post-childbearing. Triggers correspond to a positive germline panel result (variant classification per ACMG framework — pathogenic / likely pathogenic). PALB2, CHEK2, ATM, RAD51C/D, BRIP1 confirmed carriers receive distinct (gene-specific) protocols and would be authored as separate confirmed-carrier RFs in future v0.2-B iterations (out of scope here per task allowlist).
