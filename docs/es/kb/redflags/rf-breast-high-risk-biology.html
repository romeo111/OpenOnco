Notas

Biomarker-driven branching is the dominant breast-cancer pattern. Each positive biomarker opens distinct targeted-therapy options: BRCA → olaparib/talazoparib; PIK3CA → alpelisib; HER2-amp → HER2- targeted agents; PD-L1 → pembrolizumab + chemo; ESR1 → elacestrant. The Algorithm decision_tree resolves these per disease state.
