Notas

MSI-H/dMMR ~5% of metastatic CRC, ~15% of stage II-III. Adjuvant setting — ICI not yet standard outside trials (NICHE/NICHE-2 promising for neoadjuvant ICI in non-metastatic dMMR colon — emerging paradigm). Lynch-syndrome-driven dMMR cases warrant germline testing + family counseling.
