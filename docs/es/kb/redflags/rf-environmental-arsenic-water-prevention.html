Origen de la bandera roja

DefiniciónChronic ingestion of arsenic-contaminated drinking water with inorganic arsenic >10 µg/L (WHO Guideline + US EPA MCL since 2006; prior US standard was 50 µg/L). Inorganic arsenic is an IARC Group 1 carcinogen (Monograph 100C, 2012; reaffirms Monograph 84) — established etiologic driver of urothelial / bladder cancer, lung cancer (independent of smoking), and non-melanoma skin cancers (BCC + SCC + Bowen's disease). Endemic high-arsenic groundwater regions: Bangladesh, parts of India + Pakistan, Taiwan, northern Argentina / Chile, US Southwest (private wells), Mongolia, parts of Eastern Europe (including localized Ukrainian rural wells). Dose-response is supralinear at high exposures + uncertain at lower (<100 µg/L) exposures with continued risk demonstrated by cohort studies.
