Origen de la bandera roja

DefiniciónCancer survivor with prior cumulative cyclophosphamide exposure ≥7 g/m² (or ≥30 g lifetime in adults). Most commonly: high-dose regimens in non-Hodgkin lymphoma (Hyper-CVAD, dose-escalated CHOP variants), pediatric ALL induction-consolidation, bone-marrow transplant conditioning (Cy-TBI, Cy-Bu), autoimmune disease cycling pre-rituximab era. Two principal late-effect second-primary domains: (1) bladder cancer — acrolein metabolite causes chronic urothelial damage → urothelial carcinoma; SIR 4-7× population baseline, cumulative incidence 3-5% at 25y post-exposure, latency typically 10-20y; (2) therapy-related AML / MDS — cyclophosphamide is an alkylator; t-MDS / t-AML peak 5-10y post-exposure, cumulative ~1-3% at 10y.
