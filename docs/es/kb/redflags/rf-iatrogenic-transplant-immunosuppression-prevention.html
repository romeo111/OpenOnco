Origen de la bandera roja

DefiniciónChronic immunosuppression for solid-organ transplant or post-allogeneic HSCT. Elevated risk of: post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder (PTLD, EBV-driven), squamous cell carcinoma + basal cell carcinoma (skin), Kaposi sarcoma (HHV-8), non-melanoma + melanoma skin cancers, anal cancer (HPV-driven), cervical cancer, oropharyngeal cancer, HCC (HBV/HCV reactivation in transplant context).
