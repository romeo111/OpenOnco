Origen de la bandera roja

DefiniciónBaseline organ dysfunction precluding standard MCL induction (Nordic R-MaxiCHOP/AraC, R-DHAP, R-CHOP, BR): LVEF <50% (R-CHOP / R-DHAP anthracycline contraindicated), CrCl <50 (HD-cytarabine in Nordic / R-DHAP requires renal function — neurotoxicity risk; CrCl <30 absolute contraindication), bilirubin >3× ULN, severe cardiac dysfunction (ibrutinib relative contraindication), or active bleeding diathesis.

Notas

HD-cytarabine (>2 g/m² per dose in Nordic, R-DHAP) is the cerebellar-toxicity-limiting drug — CrCl <50 mandates dose reduction (1 g/m² instead of 2-3 g/m²); CrCl <30 absolute contraindication. LVEF <50%: avoid R-CHOP backbone — use BR (StiL NHL1 — bendamustine + R, no anthracycline); for fit younger transplant candidates with LVEF 40-50%, R-DHAP without anthracycline acceptable as transplant bridge. Cardiac comorbidity + intended BTKi: zanubrutinib / acalabrutinib preferred over ibrutinib (lower AF / HTN per ALPINE / ELEVATE-RR data).
