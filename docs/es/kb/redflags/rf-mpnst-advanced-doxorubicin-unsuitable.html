Notas

MPNST 1L is doxorubicin + ifosfamide (EORTC 62012). Cumulative anthracycline cap traditionally 450 mg/m²; 350 is conservative trigger. NF1-MPNST often young — congenital cardiac issues warrant baseline echo before regimen.
