Origen de la bandera roja

DefiniciónDetectable or rising plasma EBV-DNA (BIO-EBV-DNA, quantitative PCR) after completion of definitive treatment (concurrent chemoradiation +/- induction, or systemic 1L therapy) for nasopharyngeal carcinoma. Post-treatment plasma EBV-DNA is a validated prognostic/surveillance biomarker in NPC — persistent detectability or a rising trend can precede radiographic relapse by weeks to months, and should prompt accelerated re-staging workup rather than waiting for the routine imaging interval.
