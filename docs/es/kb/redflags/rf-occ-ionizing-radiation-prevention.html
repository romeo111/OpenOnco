Origen de la bandera roja

DefiniciónDocumented historical occupational exposure to ionizing radiation — typical high-risk occupations include radiologic technologists, interventional-radiology / interventional-cardiology staff, nuclear- industry workers (reactor operations, fuel-cycle, decommissioning), uranium miners (in a separate category from radon-only exposure; uranium miners receive mixed alpha + gamma exposure), industrial- radiography workers, medical-physics personnel, and military personnel with documented occupational radiation exposure — in an individual without a current radiation-attributable malignancy diagnosis. Notas

Prevention-persona RedFlag — v0.3 occupational batch-2 pilot. Fires when patient profile carries documented ≥10 years occupational ionizing-radiation exposure AND no confirmed radiation-attributable malignancy diagnosis. Engine routes to 2-track PreventionPlan: (a) IND-OCC-IONIZING-RADIATION-PREVENTION-INTENSIFIED-SURVEILLANCE (standard) — CBC q1y for hematologic surveillance; thyroid ultrasound q3-5y; lifetime dose-equivalent tracking (review of dosimetry records); ALARA / PPE counseling for active workers; breast-cancer screening at standard guideline-onset in female workers with consideration of earlier initiation per high-dose cohort guidance. (b) IND-OCC-IONIZING-RADIATION-PREVENTION-OBSERVATION (surveillance) — routine general-population screening only; patient declines structured CBC + thyroid surveillance. Trigger findings are computed booleans from occupational-history intake at the patient-profile layer. Evidence base: IARC Monograph 100D (2012); Life Span Study (atomic- bomb survivors — foundational dose-response evidence); UK NRRW pooled cohort (Muirhead 2009); INWORKS pooled cohort (Richardson 2015).
