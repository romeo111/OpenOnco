Notas

Parathyroid carcinoma has no standard cytotoxic regimen with consistent activity; this RF exists to surface the one biomarker- gated systemic option (pembrolizumab, tissue-agnostic MSI-H/dMMR + TMB-high FDA approvals) for the rare recurrent/metastatic case where it applies. No parathyroid-carcinoma-specific prevalence data exists for MSI-H/dMMR or TMB-H given disease rarity — trigger and rationale are extrapolated from general solid-tumor basket-trial eligibility, matching the pattern already used by RF-CRC-MSI-H-ACTIONABILITY and RF-CHOLANGIOCARCINOMA-HIGH-RISK-BIOLOGY for the same biomarkers. STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff.
