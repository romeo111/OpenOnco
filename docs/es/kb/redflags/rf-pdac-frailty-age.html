Origen de la bandera roja

DefiniciónFrailty profile precluding FOLFIRINOX in PDAC: ECOG ≥2, OR (age ≥75 + Charlson ≥3), OR composite (age ≥70 + albumin <3.0 + cachexia ≥10% weight loss). Triggers gem-nab-pac (better-tolerated alternative) OR gemcitabine monotherapy OR best supportive care.

Notas

ACCORD-11 excluded ECOG ≥2; FOLFIRINOX in real-world frail patients has Grade 3-4 toxicity ~75% + early discontinuation. MPACT trial for gem-nab-pac was more permissive (KPS 70-100); better fit for the borderline-fit patient. Pure BSC for ECOG 4 or organ failure.
