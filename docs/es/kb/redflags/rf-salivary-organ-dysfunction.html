Origen de la bandera roja

DefiniciónBaseline organ dysfunction precluding standard cisplatin-based chemotherapy or affecting targeted therapy in advanced salivary gland carcinoma: CrCl <60 mL/min (cisplatin contraindicated, switch to carboplatin), LVEF <50% (trastuzumab contraindicated for HER2+ SDC, doxorubicin in adenoid-cystic chemo), bilirubin >3× ULN (taxane / TKI hepatic clearance), baseline ototoxicity (cisplatin contraindicated), or QTc >480 ms (entrectinib / tipifarnib QT signal).

Notas

Cisplatin-based chemotherapy in salivary gland cancer has only modest activity (ORR ~30% in mucoepidermoid / adenoid cystic) — the organ- dysfunction flag pushes harder toward histology-specific targeted therapy when biomarker-positive. HER2+ SDC: trastuzumab + chemotherapy requires baseline LVEF ≥50% (analogous to breast cancer protocols). Larotrectinib / entrectinib for NTRK+ MASC well-tolerated; entrectinib has documented QT prolongation. Tipifarnib (HRAS+ SDC) carries cytopenias + QT prolongation. AR-targeted therapy (bicalutamide + LHRH) generally well-tolerated even with organ dysfunction — a viable de-escalation path for AR+ SDC.
