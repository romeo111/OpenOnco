Origen de la bandera roja

DefiniciónHeavy alcohol use — ≥3 standard drinks per day in men, ≥2 standard drinks per day in women (one US-standard drink = 14 g pure ethanol). IARC Monograph 100E (2012) and the IARC Working Group on Alcoholic Beverages and Ethanol classify alcoholic beverages as Group 1 carcinogen with sufficient evidence for ≥7 cancer types: oral cavity, pharynx, larynx, oesophagus (squamous cell), liver (HCC), colorectum, and female breast. WCRF / AICR Continuous Update Project (2018) adds gastric cardia and probable evidence for pancreas. Dose-response is supra-linear for upper-aerodigestive cancers (multiplicative synergy with tobacco — combined heavy use 10-100× baseline risk for oral / oesophageal cancers) and approximately log-linear for breast (HR ~1.05-1.10 per drink/day). No safe lower threshold for cancer risk has been established (ESC 2018, ICRP 2020). Universal RedFlag (relevant_diseases ["*"]) —...
