Notas

STUB pending Clinical Co-Lead sign-off (CHARTER §6.1). Models the concurrent-cisplatin backbone of RTOG 91-11 (Forastiere NEJM 2003): cisplatin 100 mg/m² IV days 1, 22, 43 concurrent with 70 Gy / 35 fx definitive RT (see RC-LARYNGEAL-LA-CRT-RTOG9111). Carboplatin substitution for cisplatin-ineligible patients is NOT formally RTOG-91-11-validated — flagged explicitly in dose_adjustments rather than presented as trial-proven.
