Notas

Standard 2L mCRC after FOLFOX progression. Adding bevacizumab (TML / ML18147 trial) improves PFS (5.7 vs 4.1 mo) + OS (11.2 vs 9.8 mo, HR 0.81) over FOLFIRI alone. Anti-EGFR (cetuximab or panitumumab) alternative add-on for RAS-WT left-sided patients in 2L if not used in 1L (or if patient progressed on bevacizumab). UGT1A1 testing recommended before high-dose irinotecan use. Cholinergic syndrome vs delayed diarrhea — distinct pathophysiology + management.
