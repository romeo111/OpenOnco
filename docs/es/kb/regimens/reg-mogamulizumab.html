Notas

MAVORIC trial (Kim 2018, Lancet Oncol): mogamulizumab 28% ORR vs vorinostat 5% ORR in r/r MF/Sézary; PFS HR 0.53. Best response in Sézary syndrome (blood B2). NCCN cat 1 for r/r; emerging 1L for advanced Sézary syndrome where access permits. Skin AE management (drug rash ~25%, distinguishing from progression critical) requires dermatology partnership — not stand-alone hematology workflow.
