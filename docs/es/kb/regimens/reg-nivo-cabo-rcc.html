Notas

CheckMate-9ER: nivo+cabo vs sunitinib, PFS 16.6 vs 8.3 mo (HR 0.51), mOS 37.7 vs 34.3 mo (HR 0.70), ORR 55.7% vs 27.1%. Cabo dose 40 mg (not 60 mg monotherapy dose) in combination. Benefit across all IMDC risk groups. Tolerable safety profile relative to other ICI+TKI combos; preferred when MET pathway co-activation is suspected or in papillary histology (off-label, but biological rationale via MET). Max nivolumab duration 2 years; cabozantinib continues until progression.
