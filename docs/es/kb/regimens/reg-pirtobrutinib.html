Notas

BRUIN trial (Mato 2023): r/r CLL/SLL after prior cBTKi (median 3 prior lines, ~80% with prior cBTKi, ~50% with prior BCL2i) — ORR 73%; in BTK-C481-mutated subset ORR ~71%; mPFS ~19.6 mo. Approved 3L+ post-cBTKi + post-BCL2i in CLL/SLL. Distinct binding mechanism (non-covalent, C481-independent) overcomes resistance to ibrutinib / acalabrutinib / zanubrutinib. Particularly active in TP53-mutated CLL where conventional chemoimmuno + chemoSCT fail.
