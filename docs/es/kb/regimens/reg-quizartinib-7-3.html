Notas

Approved 2023 for newly-dx FLT3-ITD+ AML aged 18-75. Median OS 31.9 vs 15.1 mo per QuANTUM-First. Quizartinib does NOT cover FLT3-TKD/D835 mutations — for those use midostaurin + 7+3 or refer to clinical trial. Consider alloHCT in CR1 for FLT3-ITD with high allelic burden (NPM1-wt).
