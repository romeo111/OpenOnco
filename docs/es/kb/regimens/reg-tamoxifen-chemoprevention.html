Notas

Tamoxifen 20 mg PO daily × 5 years is the FDA-approved breast-cancer chemoprevention regimen per NSABP P-1 (Fisher 1998) and IBIS-I (Cuzick). Applicable to BOTH premenopausal and postmenopausal women with elevated 5-year Gail Model risk (≥1.66%) or BRCA1/2 / strong family history. ~50% reduction in invasive breast cancer; absolute benefit varies by baseline risk. Counsel on VTE and endometrial-cancer risks; baseline pelvic + breast assessment before initiation. Postmenopausal women may prefer raloxifene (STAR P-2) or AI (MAP.3 / IBIS-II) for lower endometrial / VTE risk.
