Notas

First and only ADC approved for recurrent / metastatic cervical cancer 2L+ post-platinum (with or without bevacizumab + pembrolizumab in 1L per CPS). Pivotal innovaTV 204 (Coleman Lancet Oncol 2021, n=101): ORR 24%, mDOR 8.3 mo, mPFS 4.2 mo, mOS 12.1 mo → FDA accelerated approval Sep 20, 2021. Confirmatory innovaTV 301 (Vergote NEJM 2024, n=502, tisotumab vedotin vs investigator-choice chemo): mOS 11.5 vs 9.5 mo (HR 0.70, p=0.0038), mPFS 4.2 vs 2.9 mo (HR 0.67), ORR 17.8% vs 5.2% → FDA full approval Apr 29, 2024. Mandatory baseline ophthalmologic exam + per-cycle exams for first 9 cycles. No companion biomarker required (TF broadly expressed on cervical carcinoma). Bibliographic record SRC-INNOVATV-204 (Coleman 2021) is available; SRC-INNOVATV-301 and SRC-FDA-TIVDAK remain source gaps. Clinical claim linkage requires review.
