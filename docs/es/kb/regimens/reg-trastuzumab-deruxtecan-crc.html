Notas

DESTINY-CRC01 Phase II (2021): HER2-amp RAS-WT chemo-refractory mCRC (median 4 prior lines), ORR 45% in IHC 3+/IHC 2+ ISH+ cohort, mPFS 6.9 mo, mOS 15.5 mo. T-DXd is an ADC: trastuzumab + cleavable linker + DXd (DNA topo-I inhibitor exatecan derivative). Bystander effect benefits HER2-heterogeneous tumors. ILD/pneumonitis is the dominant safety signal (~6% all-grade, ~3% Grade ≥3) — baseline + serial lung imaging mandatory. CRC dose 6.4 mg/kg differs from breast/gastric 5.4 mg/kg (DESTINY-CRC01 protocol).
