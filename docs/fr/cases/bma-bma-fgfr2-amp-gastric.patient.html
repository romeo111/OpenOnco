Ce que cela signifie pour le traitement

A) Standard option

FOLFOX + Nivolumab

Duration (original source text): Until progression / unacceptable toxicity (chemo backbone capped at ~12 cycles in some protocols; nivo continues mono until 2 years); cycle — 14 days

Expected tolerability class:

High burden (an intensive regimen)

This is an intensive regimen that usually requires more frequent monitoring and support. This is an approximate class for the whole regimen, not a prediction for you personally: tolerability depends on your health, doses and supportive treatment.

Trastuzumab

Original source explanation; translation unavailable: Препарат-мітка для пухлин з HER2-amplification (рак грудей і шлунка). Прикріплюється до HER2-білка на пухлинній клітині та зупиняє її ріст. Контролюємо роботу серця ехокардіограмою кожні 3 місяці.

Medicine form: intravenous infusion (a drip) at the clinic.

Free under the Ukrainian NHSU programme

Capecitabine

A prodrug tablet that is converted into 5-FU in the body and blocks tumour DNA synthesis. A convenient form for taking at home. Redness and pain in the palms and soles may occur (hand-foot syndrome).

Medicine form: tablets taken by mouth.

Free under the Ukrainian NHSU programme
