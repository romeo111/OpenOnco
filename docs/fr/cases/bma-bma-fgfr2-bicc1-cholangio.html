FGFR2 fusion in intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma (~10-15%): pemigatinib (FIGHT-202, Abou-Alfa Lancet Oncol 2020 — ORR 36%, mDOR 9.1 mo) and futibatinib (FOENIX-CCA2, Goyal NEJM 2023 — ORR 42%, mPFS 9.0 mo) are FDA-approved for previously-treated FGFR2-fusion cholangio. Futibatinib is irreversible and has activity against pemigatinib-resistance gatekeeper mutations.pemigatinib monotherapy (2L+ post-gemcitabine/cisplatin)
