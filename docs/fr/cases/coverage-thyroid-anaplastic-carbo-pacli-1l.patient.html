A) Option principale dans ce rapport

Paclitaxel + carboplatin (ATC, palliative 1L)

Durée (texte de la source originale) : 6 cycles or until progression / toxicity; cycle — 21 days

Expected tolerability class:

Moderate burden

This regimen can noticeably affect how you feel and requires planned monitoring. This is an approximate class for the whole regimen, not a prediction for you personally: tolerability depends on your health, doses and supportive treatment.

blocks microtubules in a different way (paclitaxel, docetaxel). Dabrafenib
