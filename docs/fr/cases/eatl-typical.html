Etiological driver

Etiological driver · etiologically_driven archetype
Lymphome entéropathique associé aux cellules T
  • Association des maladies cœliaques — HLA-DQ2,5 / DQ8 en ~95%
  • Gluten-propulsé (celiaque réfractaire de type II → transformation EATL)
  • JAK1 / STAT3 mutations fréquentes
  • Perte de CD8 + phénotype négatif CD56

Ce qu'il ne faut pas faire

Explicit prohibitive rules, each grounded in a regimen / supportive care / contraindication entity
Standard plan (IND-EATL-1L-CHOEP-AUTOSCT)
  • Do not prescribe without a discussion of gluten-free diet — extremely important for refractory celiac patients.
  • Ne sautez pas l'abdomen CT initial pour évaluer les lésions multifocales + risque de perforation.
  • Ne négligez pas l'évaluation nutritionnelle pré-induction — les pt celiques sont souvent mal nourris.
  • Ne pas administrer de vincristine par voie intrathécale, fatale.
  • Do not give full-dose anthracycline if LVEF <50%.
  • Ne sautez pas le GCSF prophylactique — le risque d'entérocolite neutropénique augmente dans les LTEA.

RéférenceBefore starting CHOEP21TEST-CBC, TEST-CMP, TEST-LFT, TEST-LDH, TEST-HBV-SEROLOGY, TEST-ECHO
  • Assess cardiac function before anthracycline treatment with ECHO or an appropriate alternative and ECG; tailor subsequent monitoring to risk and symptoms.

Sur traitementBefore each 21-day cycle and prior to administrations as clinically indicated; etoposide is administered on days 1-3TEST-CBC
  • Repeat CBC before each cycle; monitor renal function, electrolytes, liver tests, LDH and blood glucose throughout treatment as clinically indicated.
  • For ANC <1.0 × 10^9/L and/or platelets <75 × 10^9/L, eviQ CHOEP21 states dose modification is not generally indicated; consider treatment delay and/or G-CSF after clinical assessment. Do not import fixed R-CHOP count thresholds.
  • Assess neuropathy before each treatment; clinically significant toxicity requires review of vincristine. Monitor blood glucose closely in patients with diabetes receiving corticosteroids.
  • Assess tumour-lysis risk and the prophylaxis plan. Fever or suspected infection requires urgent clinical assessment.
Fin du traitementAfter completion of the prescribed course; cardiac follow-up according to risk—
  • Arrange post-anthracycline cardiac monitoring according to risk. eviQ recommends ECHO within 12 months in all adults, with earlier monitoring for high-risk patients; this is not a fixed six-cycle treatment rule.

Mémoire sur le traitement des données à caractère personnel

Questions de discussion (3, 2 blocage)

  • BLOCKING OQ-HBV-SEROLOGY
    La sérologie du VHB (HBsAg, anti-HBc total) a-t-elle été réalisée? L'état doit être connu avant de commencer le traitement anti-CD20.
    Anti-CD20 without HBV prophylaxis in HBsAg+/anti-HBc+ patients carries significant reactivation risk (CI-HBV-NO-PROPHYLAXIS).
    → infectious_disease_hepatology
  • BLOCKING OQ-CD20-CONFIRMATION
    Is CD20+ status confirmed by histology (IHC)? Without CD20+, rituximab/obinutuzumab are not indicated.
    Anti-CD20 therapy is the backbone of most lines of treatment; absence of CD20 expression fully changes the regimen.
    → pathologiste
  • OQ-STAGING-COMPLETE
    Has complete staging been done (Lugano + PET/CT or CT)?
    Le pronostic et la sélection des pistes dépendent du poids du stade et de la tumeur.
    → radiologue
