A) Option principale dans ce rapport

MATRix (HD-Methotrexate + HD-Cytarabine + Thiotepa + Rituximab), 4 cycles

Durée (texte de la source originale) : 4 cycles induction; followed by autoSCT consolidation (BCNU + thiotepa); cycle — 21 days

Expected tolerability class:

High burden (an intensive regimen)

This is an intensive regimen that usually requires more frequent monitoring and support. This is an approximate class for the whole regimen, not a prediction for you personally: tolerability depends on your health, doses and supportive treatment.

Vérification du contenu clinique en attente

Methotrexate

blocks tumour DNA synthesis (5-FU, methotrexate, gemcitabine). Explication de la source originale ; traduction indisponible : Хіміотерапія, що блокує синтез ДНК пухлини. Залежно від дози використовується внутрішньовенно, у м'язи або у спинномозковий канал. Після високих доз потрібен «порятунок» лейковорином.

Medicine form: intravenous infusion (a drip) at the clinic.

Free under the Ukrainian NHSU programme

Cytarabine

Chemotherapy that blocks DNA synthesis in tumour cells. A treatment foundation for acute leukaemias; given intravenously or under the skin in courses.

Medicine form: intravenous infusion (a drip) at the clinic.

NHSU covers this medicine, but not for this diagnosis

Thiotepa

conventional chemotherapy that damages tumour DNA

Medicine form: intravenous infusion (a drip) at the clinic.

Free under the Ukrainian NHSU programme

Rituximab

an antibody that finds B cells and signals the immune system to destroy them, including lymphoma cells. Explication de la source originale ; traduction indisponible : Препарат-мітка, що знаходить B-клітини імунної системи (включно з пухлинними B-клітинами вашої лімфоми) і допомагає вашому організму їх знищити. Вводиться внутрішньовенно або підшкірно у складі схем для лімфом і CLL.

Medicine form: intravenous infusion (a drip) at the clinic.

Free under the Ukrainian NHSU programme

What to ask your doctor

  • Should I get a second opinion from a molecular oncologist before starting treatment?
  • How many treatment cycles will I need? How will we know whether treatment is working?
  • Which side effects are most likely for me? Which ones can I prevent?
  • Are there support groups or a psychologist for people with cancer that I could see?
  • Is this medicine available in my region? If not, how can I obtain it?
  • Why do you recommend this plan rather than the alternative?
