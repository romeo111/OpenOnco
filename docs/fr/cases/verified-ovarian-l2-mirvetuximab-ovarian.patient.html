Ce que cela signifie pour le traitement

A) Standard option

Mirvetuximab soravtansine (ovar résistant au platine, MIRASOL)

Duration (original source text): Until progression / unacceptable toxicity; cycle — 21 days

Expected tolerability class:

Moderate burden

This regimen can noticeably affect how you feel and requires planned monitoring. This is an approximate class for the whole regimen, not a prediction for you personally: tolerability depends on your health, doses and supportive treatment.

Awaiting review by doctors

Mirvetuximab soravtansine

Original source explanation; translation unavailable: Препарат-ADC для раку яєчників: знаходить FRα-позитивні клітини й доставляє їм хіміо-вантаж. Контролюємо зір — можливі тимчасові порушення з боку ока.

Medicine form: intravenous infusion (a drip) at the clinic.

Not registered in Ukraine

Duration (original source text): 6 induction cycles, then bevacizumab maintenance until progression / unacceptable toxicity; cycle — 28 days

Expected tolerability class:

High burden (an intensive regimen)

This is an intensive regimen that usually requires more frequent monitoring and support. This is an approximate class for the whole regimen, not a prediction for you personally: tolerability depends on your health, doses and supportive treatment.

Awaiting review by doctors
