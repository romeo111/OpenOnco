Résumé des faitsBRAF V600E in metastatic CRC: encorafenib + cetuximab (with or without binimetinib) improves OS vs cetuximab+irinotecan in 2L+ (BEACON-CRC, Kopetz et al. 2019). BRAFi monotherapy is ineffective in CRC due to EGFR-mediated feedback reactivation — distinct from melanoma where BRAFi alone works.
