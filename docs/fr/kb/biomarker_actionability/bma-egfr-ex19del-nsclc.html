Résumé des faitsEGFR exon 19 deletion in advanced NSCLC: osimertinib 1L improves OS vs first-generation EGFR-TKIs (FLAURA, Soria et al. 2018; Ramalingam 2020). Adjuvant osimertinib post-resection improves DFS (ADAURA, Wu et al. 2020). Exon 19 del is one of two classical sensitizing mutations (with L858R) and predicts the highest TKI response rates.
