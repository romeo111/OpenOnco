Résumé des faitsFLT3-ITD in newly-diagnosed AML: midostaurin + 7+3 induction + high-dose cytarabine consolidation + maintenance improved OS vs chemo alone in fit adults (RATIFY, Stone NEJM 2017 — 4-yr OS HR 0.78). Quizartinib + 7+3 + maintenance also superior in QuANTUM-First (Erba Lancet 2023 — 4-yr OS HR 0.78). FLT3-ITD remains a poor-risk marker (ELN 2022) when allelic ratio high; allo-SCT in CR1 indicated.

Notes

ESCAT IA. OncoKB Level 1. ELN 2022: high-allelic-ratio FLT3-ITD without NPM1 = adverse risk; FLT3-ITD with NPM1 (any AR) = intermediate. Companion diagnostic: LeukoStrat CDx FLT3 mutation assay (RT-PCR-based).
