Résumé des faitsKIT exon 11 mutation in GIST: imatinib 400 mg/day is standard 1L (B2222, Demetri NEJM 2002 — ORR 67%; EORTC-62005 / S0033 confirmed long-term benefit). Exon 11 mutants have the longest PFS and OS on imatinib of any GIST genotype. Adjuvant imatinib 3 yr post- resection improves RFS in high-risk disease (SSG-XVIII / ACOSOG-Z9001).
