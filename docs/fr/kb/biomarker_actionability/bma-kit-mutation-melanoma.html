Résumé des faitsKIT mutation in mucosal / acral / chronic-sun-damaged melanoma (~15-20% of these subtypes; rare in cutaneous CSD-low): imatinib has activity (Carvajal JCO 2013; Hodi JCO 2013 — ORR 16-23% in selected KIT-mutant melanoma). Exon 11 L576P and exon 13 K642E are the most imatinib-responsive variants. Modern frontline is immunotherapy (anti-PD-1 ± anti-CTLA-4); imatinib is reserved for IO-refractory or IO-ineligible KIT-mutant disease.
