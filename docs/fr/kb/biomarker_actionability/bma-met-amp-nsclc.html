Résumé des faitsHigh-level MET amplification in NSCLC: capmatinib activity in high-amp cohort of GEOMETRY mono-1 (ORR ~29-40% at GCN ≥10). No FDA-approved drug for MET-amp alone — actionability is expansion-cohort / off-label / trial. Also a key resistance mechanism to EGFR-TKIs (post-osimertinib MET-amp → trial enrollment for amivantamab + lazertinib MARIPOSA-2 / SAVANNAH).
