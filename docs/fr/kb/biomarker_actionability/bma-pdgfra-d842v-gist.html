Résumé des faitsPDGFRA D842V in GIST is imatinib-RESISTANT (distinct from imatinib-sensitive PDGFRA non-D842V mutations). Avapritinib (NAVIGATOR phase 1/2 + VOYAGER phase 3, Heinrich Lancet Oncol 2020 / Kang JCO 2022 — ORR 88-91% in D842V GIST; treatment-naive cohort) is FDA-approved for advanced D842V GIST. Imatinib, sunitinib, regorafenib are ineffective.

Companion-diagnostic genotyping via NGS panel mandatory before avapritinib initiation. Adjuvant imatinib has NO benefit in D842V GIST — these patients should be observed post-resection.
