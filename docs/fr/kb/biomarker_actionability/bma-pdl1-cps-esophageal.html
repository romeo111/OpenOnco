Résumé des faitsPD-L1 CPS used as eligibility threshold for ICI in metastatic esophageal cancer. Esophageal SCC: CPS ≥10 — pembrolizumab + cisplatin + fluoropyrimidine 1L (KEYNOTE-590), and pembrolizumab monotherapy 2L (KEYNOTE-181 CPS≥10 subgroup). Esophageal adeno and GEJ: same CPS criteria as gastric adeno (NCCN 2025 treats GEJ adeno with gastric algorithm). Testing by IHC 22C3 pharmDx mandatory. Threshold-gated indication selection is performed by the algorithm layer (IND-ESOPH-METASTATIC-2L-PEMBRO-CPS10); this BMA entry surfaces ESCAT tier context only.
