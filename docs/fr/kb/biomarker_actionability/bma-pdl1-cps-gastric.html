Résumé des faitsPD-L1 CPS is a continuous IHC score used as an eligibility threshold for ICI-containing regimens in metastatic gastric/GEJ adenocarcinoma. Thresholds vary by regimen: CPS ≥5 — nivolumab + fluoropyrimidine + platinum (CheckMate-649; mOS 14.4 vs 11.1 mo, HR 0.71 in CPS≥5 subgroup); CPS ≥1 — nivolumab + chemo as broader population (CM-649); CPS ≥1 — pembrolizumab + trastuzumab + chemo for HER2+ disease (KEYNOTE-811, covered separately in BMA-HER2-AMP-GASTRIC). Per NCCN and ESMO 2024, PD-L1 CPS testing by IHC 22C3 pharmDx is mandatory prior to 1L treatment selection. Threshold-gated indication selection is performed by the algorithm layer (IND-GASTRIC-METASTATIC-1L-PDL1-CHEMO-ICI); this BMA entry surfaces ESCAT tier context for tumor-board discussion only.
