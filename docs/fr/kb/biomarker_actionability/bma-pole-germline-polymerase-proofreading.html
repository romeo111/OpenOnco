Résumé des faitsPOLE germline pathogenic variants in the exonuclease (proofreading) domain cause polymerase proofreading–associated polyposis (PPAP) — characterized by attenuated/oligo-polyposis (typically <100 adenomas), early-onset colorectal cancer, elevated endometrial cancer risk in women, and characteristic ultramutator tumor phenotype (TMB >100 mut/Mb) with potential immunotherapy responsiveness. Confirmed-carrier surveillance protocol (Lynch-like; per NCCN Genetic/Familial CRC 2025): colonoscopy q1-2y starting age 20-25, EGD q3y from age 30, endometrial sampling + TVUS q1-2y from age 30-35 in women, urinalysis annual from 30 (overlap with Lynch organ spectrum). The ultramutator phenotype in POLE-mutant tumors predicts immunotherapy benefit (high TMB) — pembrolizumab is FDA- approved for TMB-high (≥10 mut/Mb) advanced solid tumors regardless of tissue; POLE-mutant CRC and endometrial benefit appears substantial in small series. ESCAT IIA (predisposition + tumor-phenotype actionability).
