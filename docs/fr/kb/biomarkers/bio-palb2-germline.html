Notes

Distinguished from BRCA1/2 by lower clinician familiarity and a more recent inclusion in NCCN high-penetrance lists (added 2020). PARPi efficacy data is smaller-N (PROfound cohort B; case series) but biologically supported. EMBRACA was germline-BRCA-only; talazoparib in PALB2 is off-label / NCCN-2A category.
