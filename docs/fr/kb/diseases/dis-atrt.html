Notes

Aggressive multimodal therapy is the standard: max-safe surgical resection + adjuvant intensified chemotherapy + (in patients >3 y who tolerate) craniospinal RT. Tazemetostat (EZH2 inhibitor) is FDA-approved for relapsed/refractory adult and pediatric INI1- negative epithelioid sarcoma + ATRT (limited pediatric data). Surveillance for RTPS carriers: brain MRI q3mo from infancy to age 3-4, then q6mo to age 5; whole-body MRI q3-6mo to age 5 to detect extracranial MRT of kidney + soft tissue.
