Notes

Three subsites with distinct biology and treatment patterns. Early glottic (T1-T2N0): radiation OR transoral laser microsurgery (TLM) — both ~90% local control with voice preservation as key endpoint. Locally advanced: larynx-preservation chemoradiation (cisplatin + RT) per RTOG 91-11 / VA-trial paradigm vs total laryngectomy + postoperative RT — patient preference / fitness drives choice. Recurrent / metastatic: pembrolizumab ± chemo per KEYNOTE-048 (same framework as broader HNSCC). Smoking cessation surveillance dominates prevention narrative.
