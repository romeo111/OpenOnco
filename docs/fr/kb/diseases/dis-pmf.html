Capacités

Notes

Risk-stratified archetype using DIPSS-Plus. Clinical decision flow: (1) Transplant-eligibility assessment for intermediate-2 / high-risk; (2) symptomatic splenomegaly / constitutional symptoms → ruxolitinib (COMFORT-I/II) — momelotinib if anemia is dominant problem (MOMENTUM); fedratinib 2L (FREEDOM-2); pacritinib for severe thrombocytopenia (PERSIST). Anemia management as dedicated track (transfusion, ESA in selected, danazol). NEVER abrupt-discontinue ruxolitinib (cytokine rebound). STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff before publication. Myeloid expansion (heme).
