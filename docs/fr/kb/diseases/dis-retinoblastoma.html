Capacités

Notes

Most common pediatric ocular malignancy. Germline RB1 testing mandatory at diagnosis — drives sibling cascade screening, lifetime second-primary surveillance (osteosarcoma, soft-tissue sarcomas, melanoma; ~50% lifetime risk of second cancer in germline carriers), and treatment choice (avoid external-beam RT in germline carriers because of post-RT sarcoma risk in field). Treatment: intra-arterial melphalan chemo (Group A-B-C eyes) is modern standard with high eye-salvage rates; enucleation for Group D-E or RT-induced cataract / glaucoma; systemic chemo (carboplatin + etoposide + vincristine) for bilateral / disseminated disease.
