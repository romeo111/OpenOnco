Capacités

Notes

Clinical hallmark: massive splenomegaly + cytopenias from hypersplenism (often with little lymphadenopathy). HCV testing MANDATORY at diagnosis — DAA can produce durable lymphoma regression. For HCV-negative, rituximab monotherapy or splenectomy are 1L options.
