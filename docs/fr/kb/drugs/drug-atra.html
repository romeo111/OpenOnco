Faits sur les drogues

CatégorieRétinoïde (dérivé vitaline A); agent de différenciation
MécanismeBinds retinoic-acid receptor α (RARα) and degrades the PML-RARα fusion oncoprotein in APL → forces blast differentiation rather than killing. Cornerstone of every APL induction regimen.
Dose typiqueAPL: 45 mg/m²/day PO divided BID until complete remission (typically ~30-50 days). Continue per consolidation/maintenance protocol. Initiate IMMEDIATELY at clinical APL suspicion (don't wait for cytogenetic confirmation) — mortality from bleeding > from leukemia in first 7 days.

Avertissements

  • Differentiation syndrome (formerly RA-syndrome) — fever, dyspnea, weight gain, pulmonary infiltrates, hypotension; potentially fatal; treat with dexamethasone 10 mg IV BID
  • Leucocytose — le taux élevé de CBC au diagnostic prédit un risque de SSD plus élevé; stéroïdes prophylactiques si le CBC >5K (faible risque) ou >10K (haute risque)
  • Tératogénicité — la grossesse doit être exclue

Notes

Initiate ATRA at clinical APL suspicion based on smear findings + DIC pattern, BEFORE PML-RARα confirmation — early death from bleeding is the dominant APL mortality. Differentiation-syndrome prophylaxis with dexamethasone 10 mg IV BID for high-risk (WBC >10K) or rising WBC. Pregnancy-test gating + 2 forms of contraception mandatory.
