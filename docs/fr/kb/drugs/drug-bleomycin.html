Faits sur les drogues

CatégorieAntibiotique antitumoral glycopeptide
MécanismeGenerates free radicals causing DNA strand breaks. G2 cell-cycle phase active. Lung tissue lacks bleomycin hydrolase → accumulates → pulmonary toxicity.
Dose typiqueABVD: 10 USP units/m² IV days 1+15 of 28-day cycle. Cumulative lifetime limit ~400 USP units (pulmonary toxicity).

Avertissements

  • Fibrose pulmonaire (dépendante de la dose cumulée, souvent progressive après exposition)
  • Réactions anaphylactoïdes (dose recommandée en premier)

Notes

Replaced in modern Hodgkin 1L by brentuximab vedotin (A+AVD per ECHELON-1) due to pulmonary toxicity. NEVER combine with brentuximab (additive lung toxicity). Avoid G-CSF on ABVD (increases pulmonary toxicity). Pre-treatment PFT (DLCO) baseline + serial monitoring. Avoid high-flow O2 during anesthesia post-bleomycin (lifetime).

Evidence for specific statements

These citations cover only the statements and scopes shown. They do not establish clinical approval of this record.

For bleomycin, 1 USP unit equals 1000 International Units under the eviQ convention. US product vials are labelled 15 or 30 units of Bleomycin for Injection USP. Confirm the formulation and unit system; milligram activity must not be treated as a generic mg-by-weight conversion.

Unit nomenclature and equivalence only; not individualized dosing, schedule selection, cumulative-risk elimination or whole-record clinical approval.
