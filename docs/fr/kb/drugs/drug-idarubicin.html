Faits sur les drogues

CatégorieAnthracycline (inhibiteur de la topoïsomerase II + intercalateur d'ADN)
MécanismeLipophilic anthracycline analog of daunorubicin; better intracellular penetration. Same mechanism as daunorubicin but somewhat lower cardiotoxicity per dose. Used as alternative anthracycline in AML 7+3 and as anthracycline component of high-risk APL induction (AIDA / AIDA-like schemes).

Notes

Interchangeable with daunorubicin in 7+3 (no clear OS difference in meta-analysis). Anthracycline of choice in classic AIDA APL induction. Cumulative lifetime threshold lower than daunorubicin (~150 mg/m²).
