Avertissements

  • Toxicité embryo-fotale (tératogène)
  • Toxicité hématologique (neutropénie, thrombocytopénie)
  • thromboembolie veineuse et artérielle (prophylaxie obligatoire)

Notes

Renal dose adjustment is critical: CrCl 30-60 mL/min → 10 mg daily; CrCl <30 (no dialysis) → 15 mg every other day; dialysis → 5 mg post- dialysis on dialysis days. VTE prophylaxis (aspirin 81-325 mg or LMWH in higher-risk patients) is mandatory throughout therapy.
