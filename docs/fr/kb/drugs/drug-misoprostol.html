Faits sur les drogues

CatégorieAnalogique synthétique de prostaglandine E1
MécanismeSynthetic prostaglandin E1 analog with gastric mucosal protective effects (mucus + bicarbonate secretion, decreased gastric acid via enterochromaffin-like cell inhibition) and uterotonic activity. In oncology / cancer-prevention context: investigated as endometrial protection in tamoxifen users (theoretical rationale — endometrial PGE pathways). Evidence base is limited and inconsistent; not guideline-recommended for routine tamoxifen-associated endometrial protection. Primary FDA indications are NSAID-induced peptic ulcer prophylaxis and (off-label) obstetric uses.
Dose typiqueNSAID ulcer prevention: 200 mcg PO QID with food and at bedtime (alternative: 200 mcg BID-TID for tolerability). Tamoxifen endometrial protection (off-label, research context only): 200 mcg PO regimens have been studied — NOT a guideline-supported use; clinician judgment + IRB if pursued.

Avertissements

  • Le misoprostol peut provoquer une rupture utérine, un avortement, une naissance prématurée ou des malformations congénitales — l'utilisation pendant la grossesse peut entraîner une perte de grossesse; les femmes doivent subir un test de grossesse négatif avant d'amorcer et utiliser une contraception efficace
