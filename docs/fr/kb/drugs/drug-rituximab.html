Faits sur les drogues

Catégoriemonoclonal_antibody — anti-CD20 chimeric IgG1κ
MécanismeChimeric (mouse-human) monoclonal antibody binding the CD20 antigen on B lymphocytes (pre-B through mature B; sparing pro-B and plasma cells). Depletes CD20+ B cells via three mechanisms: complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC, dominant in CLL), antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC, dominant in lymphoma), and direct apoptosis via CD20 cross-linking. Recovery of normal B cells takes 6-12 months after the last dose.
Dose typiqueR-CHOP / R-CVP / R-Bendamustine (DLBCL, FL): 375 mg/m² IV day 1 of each 21- or 28-day cycle × 6-8 cycles. R-monotherapy / FL maintenance: 375 mg/m² IV every 8-12 weeks × 2 years (PRIMA / RESORT schedules). CLL (R-FC, R-Ben): 375 mg/m² IV cycle 1 day 1, then 500 mg/m² IV day 1 cycles 2-6. SC formulation (FL, DLBCL): 1400 mg fixed-dose SC over ~5 minutes from cycle 2 onward (after first IV dose for tolerability).

Avertissements

  • Réactions fatales à la perfusion (dans les 24 h suivant la première dose; cycle 1)
  • Réactions muco-cutanées sévères (Stevens-Johnson, TEN, pémphigue paranéoplasique)
  • Réactivation du virus de l'hépatite B — insuffisance hépatique fulminante possible; dépistage de l ' AgHBs + anti-HBc avant la première dose; prophylaxie par entecavir si positif
  • Leucoencéphalopathie multifocale progressive (LMP, réactivation du virus JC)

Notes

Foundation anti-CD20 therapy in B-cell lymphomas. Premedication (acetaminophen + diphenhydramine ± methylprednisolone) before each infusion; first-dose infusion rate escalation per protocol (50 mg/h start, doubled q30min to max 400 mg/h). HBV screening (HBsAg + anti-HBc + anti-HBs) is mandatory before first dose; entecavir or tenofovir prophylaxis throughout therapy and ≥12 months post for any HBV-positive serology. SC formulation reduces administration time from 4-6 h to 5-7 minutes and is preferred from cycle 2 onward in FL / DLBCL. For HCV-MZL, reserved for cases not responding to antiviral therapy alone, or aggressive phenotype where immediate cytoreduction is indicated. Biosimilars are clinically interchangeable in UA per НСЗУ guidance.
