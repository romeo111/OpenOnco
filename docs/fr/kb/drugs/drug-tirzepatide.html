Faits sur les drogues

CatégorieDual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist (once-weekly)
MécanismeSynthetic 39-amino-acid peptide that activates both glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and GLP-1 receptors. Dual agonism produces greater glycemic control and weight loss than GLP-1 monoagonism. At obesity dose (max 15 mg weekly), SURMOUNT-1 trial demonstrated ~21% mean weight loss vs ~3% on placebo — superior to semaglutide 2.4 mg. Obesity is associated with 13+ cancers (IARC/WCRF) — sustained weight loss is a cancer-prevention intervention.
Dose typiqueSC: dose escalation over ≥20 weeks to maximize tolerability: Week 1-4: 2.5 mg SC weekly Week 5-8: 5 mg SC weekly Week 9-12: 7.5 mg SC weekly Week 13-16: 10 mg SC weekly Week 17-20: 12.5 mg SC weekly Week 21 onward: 15 mg SC weekly (maximum obesity dose) Inject SC abdomen / thigh / upper arm any day of the week; rotate sites. Lower maintenance doses (5, 10, 15 mg) acceptable based on response/tolerance.
