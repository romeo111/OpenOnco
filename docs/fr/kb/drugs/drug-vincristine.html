Avertissements

  • FATAL if administered intrathecally — must be packaged in mini-bag, never syringe, per WHO/FDA

Notes

Cap at 2 mg per dose (regardless of BSA) to limit neuropathy. Held or dose-reduced for grade ≥2 peripheral neuropathy. Hepatic dose adjustment for total bilirubin >3 mg/dL. WHO has issued repeated warnings about fatal accidental intrathecal administration — always packaged in IV mini-bag, never as a syringe.
