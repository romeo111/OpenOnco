Faits sur les drogues

CatégorieAnticorps bispécifiques HER2 × HER3 (ciblés par NRG1-fusion)
MécanismeHumanized IgG1 bispecific antibody binding HER2 (one arm) and HER3 (other arm). Mechanism of action against NRG1-fusion-driven tumors: zenocutuzumab docks on HER2, blocks the NRG1-fusion ligand from engaging HER3, disrupting HER2-HER3 heterodimerization and downstream PI3K/AKT signalling. NRG1 fusions are rare (<1%) but occur across pancreatic, NSCLC, breast, cholangiocarcinoma, and other solid tumors.
Dose typique750 mg IV q2 semaines jusqu'à progression de la maladie ou toxicité inacceptable. Prémédiquer avec antihistaminique + acétaminophène ± corticoïde pour la première perfusion.

Notes

eNRGy trial (NCT02912949): Phase II, NRG1-fusion-positive solid tumors (pan-tumor basket). NSCLC cohort: ORR 33%, mDOR 7.4 mo. Pancreatic adenocarcinoma cohort: ORR 40%, mDOR 7.4 mo. FDA accelerated approval Dec 2024 — confirmatory study required. NRG1 fusion testing requires RNA-based NGS (DNA NGS misses many fusions).
