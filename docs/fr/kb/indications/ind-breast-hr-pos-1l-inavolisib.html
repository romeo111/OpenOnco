Notes

ESCAT IA. INAVO120 (NCT04191499): inavolisib + palbociclib + fulvestrant in PIK3CA-mutant HR+/HER2- metastatic breast with endocrine-resistant disease (recurrence on/within 12 mo of completing adjuvant endocrine therapy, or de-novo metastatic). Median PFS 15.0 vs 7.3 mo (HR 0.43). FDA approval Oct 2024 (Itovebi). Mutant-selective design yields lower hyperglycemia + rash burden than alpelisib while preserving efficacy. Pre-treatment PIK3CA testing (tissue NGS or ctDNA) required.
