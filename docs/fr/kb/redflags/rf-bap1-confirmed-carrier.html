Origine du drapeau rouge

DéfinitionConfirmed germline pathogenic / likely-pathogenic variant in the BAP1 gene — BAP1 tumor predisposition syndrome (BAP1-TPDS). Patient has had germline BAP1 testing returned positive; the pedigree-suspicion question is settled. No current personal cancer diagnosis in the carrier being assessed. Mandates structured surveillance for the BAP1-spectrum cancers: uveal melanoma, cutaneous melanoma, malignant mesothelioma (pleural + peritoneal), clear-cell RCC, and atypical Spitz tumors (often as childhood / adolescent sentinels). Prevention- persona RedFlag (§20, v0.2-B confirmed-carrier surveillance pathway — distinct from RF-BAP1-FAMILY-HISTORY-SUSPICION which fires before testing).

Notes

v0.2-B confirmed-carrier surveillance pathway — BAP1-TPDS. Fires on documented germline BAP1 pathogenic variant positivity. Engine routes to PreventionPlan recommending: (a) IND-BAP1-CARRIER-SURVEILLANCE (standard) — annual whole-body skin exam by dermatologist; annual ophthalmologic exam with dilated fundoscopy for uveal melanoma; annual abdominal MRI for clear-cell RCC; chest CT q1-3y for mesothelioma surveillance; explicit awareness of atypical Spitz tumors as sentinel lesions. (b) IND-BAP1-CARRIER-INTENSIFIED (aggressive) — standard PLUS asbestos-avoidance counseling (most important modifiable cofactor for BAP1-associated mesothelioma) + earlier mesothelioma surveillance (chest CT q1y in occupational / environmental asbestos exposure). STUB pending two-Clinical-Co-Lead signoff per CHARTER §6.1 dev-mode exemption.
