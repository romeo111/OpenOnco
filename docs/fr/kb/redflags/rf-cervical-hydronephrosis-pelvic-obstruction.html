Origine du drapeau rouge

DéfinitionHydronephrosis or pelvic-mass-induced ureteric obstruction in cervical cancer (FIGO IIIB by definition). Mandates urgent renal decompression (percutaneous nephrostomy or ureteric stent) BEFORE cisplatin-based CRT — cisplatin nephrotoxic + ureteric obstruction worsens AKI.

Notes

FIGO IIIB definition includes hydronephrosis from tumor — substantial prognostic weight. Cisplatin radiosensitization needs CrCl ≥60; decompression first, restage CrCl, then proceed with CRT.
