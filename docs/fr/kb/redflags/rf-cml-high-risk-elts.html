Notes

ELN 2020 prefers ELTS over Sokal for predicting CML-related death and guiding 1L TKI selection in younger patients seeking treatment-free remission. High-risk patients gain more from 2nd-gen TKIs in early deep molecular response, but no consistent OS difference vs imatinib. Comorbidity profile (RF-CML-COMORBIDITY-COMPLEX) co-determines which 2nd-gen agent. STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff.
