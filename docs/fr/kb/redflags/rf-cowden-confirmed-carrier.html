Origine du drapeau rouge

DéfinitionConfirmed germline pathogenic / likely-pathogenic variant in the PTEN gene (Cowden syndrome / PTEN hamartoma tumor syndrome, PHTS). Patient has had germline PTEN testing returned positive; the pedigree-suspicion question is settled. No current personal cancer diagnosis in the carrier being assessed. Mandates multi-organ surveillance for the Cowden-spectrum cancers (breast — ~85% lifetime; endometrial — ~28%; thyroid — ~35%; renal — ~34%; colorectal — ~9%; melanoma — ~6%) with protocols starting variously between diagnosis and age 40 depending on organ. Prevention-persona RedFlag (§20, v0.2-B confirmed-carrier surveillance pathway — distinct from RF-COWDEN-FAMILY-HISTORY-SUSPICION which fires before testing).

Notes

v0.2-B confirmed-carrier surveillance pathway — Cowden / PHTS. Fires on documented germline PTEN pathogenic variant positivity in an asymptomatic individual. Engine routes to PreventionPlan recommending: (a) IND-COWDEN-CARRIER-SURVEILLANCE (standard) — annual breast mammography + MRI from age 30 (women); annual thyroid US from diagnosis; endometrial sampling / TVUS q1y from age 35-40 (women); colonoscopy from age 35 then per-finding; annual skin exam; renal US or MRI q1-2y from age 40. (b) IND-COWDEN-CARRIER-INTENSIFIED (aggressive) — standard protocol PLUS risk-reducing mastectomy / hysterectomy discussion post-childbearing.
