Origine du drapeau rouge

DéfinitionFrailty profile precluding standard FOLFOX+nivo / FLOT in gastric cancer: ECOG ≥3, OR (age ≥75 + Charlson ≥3), OR composite (age ≥70 + albumin <3.0 + ≥10% weight loss + sarcopenia — common in gastric cancer). Triggers regimen de-escalation (5-FU / capecitabine monotherapy, omit ICI/biologic).

Notes

Pre-treatment nutrition optimization (PEG/J-tube, parenteral nutrition bridge) often required. Nivolumab-containing regimens have lower symptomatic toxicity than FLOT for elderly — ATTRACTION-4 included ECOG 0-1 only; real-world frail elderly: capecitabine + nivolumab is reasonable de-escalation.
