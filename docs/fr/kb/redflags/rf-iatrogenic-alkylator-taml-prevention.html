Origine du drapeau rouge

DéfinitionHistory of prior cumulative alkylating-agent chemotherapy exposure (cyclophosphamide, ifosfamide, melphalan, busulfan, chlorambucil, procarbazine, bendamustine, BCNU/CCNU/lomustine, dacarbazine, temozolomide) ≥5 years prior. Therapy-related AML (t-AML) and therapy-related MDS following alkylator exposure has characteristic features: median latency 5-7 years post-exposure (longer than topoisomerase-II-inhibitor t-AML), preceded often by t-MDS phase with chromosomes 5q-/-5 or 7q-/-7 cytogenetics, complex karyotype. Cumulative incidence ~1-10% at 10y depending on dose + concurrent RT + age. Highest risk: Hodgkin lymphoma survivors (MOPP-era), multiple myeloma (melphalan), pediatric sarcoma survivors. Prevention-persona RedFlag (§20 v0.3 iatrogenic pilot).
