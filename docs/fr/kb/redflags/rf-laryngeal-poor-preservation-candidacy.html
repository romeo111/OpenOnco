Origine du drapeau rouge

DéfinitionFeature precluding larynx-preservation chemoradiation (RTOG 91-11 concurrent cisplatin + RT) as the preferred locally advanced treatment pathway, favoring primary total laryngectomy instead: radiographic cartilage invasion / destruction, tumor extension through cartilage into extralaryngeal soft tissue, substantial (≥1 cm) invasion of the tongue base, or pretreatment laryngeal dysfunction (severe aspiration, or feeding-tube / tracheostomy dependence) indicating a non-functional larynx that organ- preservation therapy would not salvage.

Notes

STUB pending Clinical Co-Lead sign-off (CHARTER §6.1). Larynx- preservation-candidacy gate for locally advanced laryngeal SCC — routes ALGO-LARYNGEAL-1L step 5 to primary total laryngectomy instead of RTOG 91-11 concurrent chemoradiation. Grounded in the Disease-level narrative's "patient preference/fitness drives choice" framing plus well-established organ-preservation-candidacy criteria (RTOG 91-11 itself excluded T4 disease with cartilage destruction / >1 cm tongue-base invasion from its organ-preservation comparison). Distinct from RF-HNSCC-ORGAN-DYSFUNCTION (reused unchanged at ALGO-LARYNGEAL-1L step 6), which gates cisplatin FITNESS rather than larynx-preservation CANDIDACY. clinical_direction set to `intensify` (not `investigate`) per independent safety-review fix: this RF escalates from organ-preservation CRT to primary radical surgery when it fires, and every other algorithm-shifting RedFlag in this KB uses intensify/de-escalate/hold, never investigate (confirmed by grepping redflags/ sibling files) — the loader's _check_redflag_contracts flags investigate+non-empty shifts_algorithm as a re-classification warning.
