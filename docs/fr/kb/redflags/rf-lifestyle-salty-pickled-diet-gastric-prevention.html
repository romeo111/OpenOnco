Origine du drapeau rouge

DéfinitionHabitual high intake of salt-preserved foods, pickled vegetables, and high-salt dishes (>5 g/day sodium chronically). IARC + WCRF/AICR evidence supports salt-preserved foods as a probable cause of gastric cancer (RR ~1.5-2.0 in high-intake vs low-intake quartiles; Ge et al. Gastroenterology 2012 meta-analysis). Mechanism: chronic mucosal irritation + Helicobacter pylori synergy + N-nitroso compound formation in salted/cured foods (kimchi, salted fish, pickled vegetables, jaroma/sauerkraut depending on preparation, soy sauce, cured meats). Particularly relevant in East Asian + Eastern European populations with high baseline H. pylori prevalence + high pickled- food consumption. WHO recommends <5 g/day salt for cancer + cardio- vascular prevention. Prevention-persona RedFlag (§20 v0.3 lifestyle expansion).
