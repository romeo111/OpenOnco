Origine du drapeau rouge

DéfinitionConfirmed germline pathogenic / likely-pathogenic variant in CDKN1B (p27/Kip1) — Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia type 4 (MEN4). Patient has had germline CDKN1B testing returned positive; the pedigree-suspicion question is settled. MEN4 is a more recently characterized (Pellegata et al. 2006) MEN1-like syndrome — autosomal dominant inheritance with overlapping but generally less penetrant tumor spectrum than classical MEN1. Tumor distribution: primary hyperparathyroidism (most common manifestation, ~80% in series), pituitary adenoma (~30-40%, often somatotrophs / corticotrophs), GEP-NET (pancreatic + duodenal + gastric — gastrinoma less common than in MEN1), adrenocortical tumors (modest), bronchial / thymic NET (rare but reported), reproductive-tract neuroendocrine tumors.
