Notes

RET M918T (MEN2B) = highest-risk MEN syndrome — prophylactic thyroidectomy in first year of life per ATA guidelines. Sporadic MTC with somatic RET M918T (~25-30%) has worse prognosis than RET-wild- type or RAS-mutant. Selpercatinib (LIBRETTO-001/-531; ORR ~70%, PFS ~25 mo in advanced RET-mutant MTC) is now standard 1L for advanced / metastatic RET-mutant MTC. Pralsetinib (ARROW) similar efficacy. RAS- mutant MTC is RET-wild-type and does NOT respond to RET inhibitors — vandetanib (ZETA) or cabozantinib (EXAM) are 1L for these. Comprehensive germline + tumor RET / RAS testing at diagnosis is now standard (NCCN Cat 1 recommendation).
