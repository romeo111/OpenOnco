Origine du drapeau rouge

DéfinitionDocumented occupational aromatic-amine exposure — benzidine, 2-naphthylamine, 4-aminobiphenyl — primarily in historical pre-regulation dye, rubber, leather, pigment, and chemical-manufacturing industries (mostly pre-1980s in regulated countries; ongoing in unregulated jurisdictions). IARC Group 1 carcinogen for bladder cancer. Engine routes to PreventionPlan recommending annual urinalysis + cytology + low-threshold cystoscopy for hematuria + smoking cessation.
