Notes

Prevention-persona RedFlag — v0.3 IARC 2A probable-carcinogen batch (2026-05-19). IARC Group 2A probable — limited human evidence; clinical recommendation more conservative than Group 1. IARC Monograph 112 (2015) is the canonical evidence anchor and is NOT yet a separate SRC entity in KB; cited in notes only. Evidence-base contestation: the IARC Group 2A classification is not concordant with EFSA / EPA / Health Canada / JMPR "unlikely to pose carcinogenic risk" conclusions. This RF is authored at severity:minor reflecting both the IARC 2A probable evidence quality AND the cross-regulatory disagreement. Counseling should disclose the controversy honestly rather than presenting IARC as settled science. Anchor scope: DLBCL only (the dominant NHL endpoint in IARC 112 limited-human-evidence summary). Other NHL subtypes (follicular, marginal zone, etc.) and other hematologic malignancies (MM, AML) carry inadequate or more-contested evidence and are deliberately excluded from relevant_diseases.
