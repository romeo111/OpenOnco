Notes

Sanz score (Sanz et al. Blood 2000) stratifies APL by presenting WBC and platelets: low = WBC ≤10 + plt >40, intermediate = WBC ≤10 + plt ≤40, high = WBC >10. APL0406 (Lo-Coco 2013, NEJM) randomized low/intermediate-risk patients to ATRA+ATO vs ATRA+chemo and showed non-inferior EFS plus reduced toxicity for ATRA+ATO. The chemo-free arm is now the standard of care for non-high-risk APL (NCCN AML 2025, ELN APL 2019). de-escalate direction reflects the avoidance of anthracycline-based consolidation.
