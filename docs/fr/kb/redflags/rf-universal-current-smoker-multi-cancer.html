Origine du drapeau rouge

DéfinitionCurrent tobacco use ≥3 months at any quantity (cigarettes, cigars, pipe, smokeless tobacco, hookah / waterpipe). IARC Monograph 100A (2012) classifies tobacco smoking as Group 1 carcinogen with sufficient evidence in humans for cancers of: oral cavity, oro- / hypo- / nasopharynx, paranasal sinuses, larynx, oesophagus, stomach, colon and rectum, liver, pancreas, lung, kidney (RCC and renal pelvis / ureter urothelial), urinary bladder, uterine cervix, ovary (mucinous), and myeloid leukaemia (≥17 sites). Smokeless tobacco adds oral / oesophageal / pancreas associations. Current-smoker status is the highest-yield intervention point in cancer prevention — dose-response is steep and cessation benefits accrue from year 1 with measurable lung-cancer risk reduction by year 5 and continuing for ≥20y.
