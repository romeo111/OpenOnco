Origine du drapeau rouge

DéfinitionEnfortumab vedotin (EV) contraindication in urothelial carcinoma — MMAE-related toxicity exclusions that preclude EV+pembrolizumab (EV-302/KEYNOTE-A39 exclusion criteria): (1) pre-existing severe peripheral neuropathy (Grade ≥2) — MMAE causes additive neuropathy; (2) uncontrolled diabetes mellitus — EV-related hyperglycemia risk; (3) severe corneal disease or active keratopathy — EV-class ocular toxicity. Fires to route EV-ineligible patients to platinum-based chemotherapy + avelumab maintenance.
