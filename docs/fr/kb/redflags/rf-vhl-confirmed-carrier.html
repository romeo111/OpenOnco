Notes

Prevention-persona confirmed-carrier RedFlag for VHL syndrome. Fires when patient profile has germline_vhl_pathogenic_variant_confirmed=true. Engine routes to PreventionPlan recommending VHL Alliance Active Surveillance protocol: MRI brain+spine q1y from age 15-16; MRI abdomen (kidneys+adrenals+pancreas) q1-2y from 15; ophthalmology q1y from age 1-5; catecholamines/metanephrines q1y from age 5-10; comprehensive audiology q2-3y. Diseases-anchored to DIS-HCC (closest in-KB hepatobiliary anchor since DIS-RCC may not be in this branch) and DIS-PHEOCHROMOCYTOMA (newly authored). STUB pending two-Clinical-Co-Lead signoff per CHARTER §6.1 dev-mode.
