These citations cover only the statements and scopes shown. They do not establish clinical approval of this record.

Bleomycin exposure warrants perioperative pulmonary-risk assessment. Label precautions favor lower oxygen concentrations, but observational evidence does not establish a universal FiO2 ceiling. Anesthesia should balance pulmonary risk with adequate oxygenation; a fixed limit must not cause inadequate oxygenation.

Perioperative oxygen management after bleomycin only; no verification of other BEP dosing or supportive-care statements.

In the 1997 MRC/EORTC randomized study of good-risk metastatic nonseminomatous germ-cell cancer, the tested carboplatin-based CEB regimen was inferior to the tested cisplatin-based BEP regimen. This does not establish an alternative regimen for severe kidney impairment.

Bleomycin unit systems must be identified explicitly. The eviQ equivalence is 1.5 USP units = 1500 International Units, so 30 USP units = 30000 International Units. Unit nomenclature and equivalence only; not individualized dosing, schedule selection, cumulative-risk elimination or whole-record clinical approval.
