Notes

CaRD trial (Treon 2014): r/r WM — major response rate 87%, VGPR 68%; particularly active in CXCR4-WHIM-mutated subset where BTKi response weaker. Carfilzomib (irreversible proteasome inhibitor) has minimal peripheral neuropathy vs bortezomib — preferred for WM IgM-related neuropathy patients. Cardiac monitoring critical — carfilzomib has cardiotoxicity signal (CHF, hypertension) particularly in older patients. Weekly schedule less cardiotoxic but lower efficacy data in WM.
