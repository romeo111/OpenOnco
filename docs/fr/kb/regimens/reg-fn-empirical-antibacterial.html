Notes

IDSA 2010 + ASCO 2018 + MASCC 2017 + NCCN Prevention and Treatment of Cancer-Related Infections 2024 standard. FN = single oral temperature ≥38.3°C OR ≥38°C sustained ≥1h, AND ANC <500 (or expected to fall <500 within 48h). Empirical anti-pseudomonal beta-lactam mono-therapy is the BACKBONE: piperacillin-tazobactam 4.5g IV q8h (extended infusion 4h preferred for severe sepsis), OR cefepime 2g IV q8h, OR meropenem 1g IV q8h (if prior ESBL / resistant GN risk). Vancomycin NOT routine — added only for MRSA-risk subsets. Time-to- antibiotic <60 min from FN recognition is critical (mortality surrogate). Source-control imperative: blood cultures × 2 (peripheral + central line), urine, sputum, stool C. diff if diarrhea, CT chest early if respiratory symptoms. G-CSF recommended for high-risk + sepsis/instability (does not replace antibiotics). Daily reassessment for de-escalation, antifungal addition, source localization. This regimen entity is the UMBRELLA — actual prescribing requires institutional antibiogram + clinical judgment + per-patient customization. Many supporting DRUG entities missing — flagged.
