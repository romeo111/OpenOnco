Notes

QUAZAR AML-001 (Wei 2020): oral azacitidine 300 mg PO daily × 14 d / 28-d cycle vs placebo as maintenance after CR/CRi post-induction ± consolidation in AML patients ≥55 years not eligible for HCT. mOS 24.7 vs 14.8 months (HR 0.69, p<0.001), mRFS 10.2 vs 4.8 months. FDA-approved Sep 2020 as Onureg. Critical: oral CC-486 is NOT bioequivalent to SC/IV azacitidine — only the oral formulation has this maintenance indication. Ukraine access major barrier (not registered as Onureg). STUB — requires Clinical Co-Lead signoff.
