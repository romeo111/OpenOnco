Notes

RAINBOW trial (Wilke 2014): 2L gastric/GEJ adenocarcinoma post-platinum/ fluoropyrimidine — paclitaxel + ramucirumab vs paclitaxel mOS 9.6 vs 7.4 mo (HR 0.81), PFS 4.4 vs 2.9 mo, ORR 28% vs 16%. Established as preferred 2L regimen per NCCN cat 1. REGARD trial showed single-agent ramucirumab benefit but combination preferred when tolerated. Hypertension + bleeding monitoring + 28-day pre/post-surgery hold mandatory.
