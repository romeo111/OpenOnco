Notes

KEYNOTE-204 trial (Kuruvilla 2021): r/r cHL post-BV (n=304) — pembrolizumab vs BV — mPFS 13.2 vs 8.3 mo (HR 0.65, p=0.00271); CR rate 24.5% vs 24.2% (similar); PFS benefit predominantly from durable PR/SD plateau. Established 2L+ for r/r cHL post-BV; also useful in BV-naïve r/r cHL based on KEYNOTE-087. cHL has high PD-L1 expression (9p24.1 amplification) → strong checkpoint responses. Bridge to alloSCT in fit transplant-eligible responders per SCT-eligible practice. Combined with BV (BV+pembro) being studied as 1L in Hodgkin Lymphoma Study Group (HD21-style) combinations.
