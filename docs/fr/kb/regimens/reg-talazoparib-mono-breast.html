Notes

EMBRACA (NCT01945775): Phase III, gBRCA1/2-mutant HER2- locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer (≤3 prior cytotoxic regimens for advanced disease). Talazoparib 1 mg PO daily vs physician's- choice single-agent chemo (capecitabine / eribulin / gemcitabine / vinorelbine). Median PFS 8.6 vs 5.6 mo (HR 0.54); ORR 62.6% vs 27.2%. OS not significantly different at final analysis (HR 0.85, p=0.17). FDA approval Oct 2018. Greater PARP-trapping potency than olaparib — anemia is dose-limiting AE (~53% any grade, ~39% G≥3); transfusion support frequently required. Germline BRCA1/2 testing (peripheral blood) required pre-start. Monotherapy alternative to olaparib OlympiAD; head-to-head not done.
