Notes

TOGA trial 2010 — first targeted therapy approval in metastatic gastric. mOS 13.8 vs 11.1 chemo-only; HER2 IHC 3+ subgroup mOS 17.9. KEYNOTE-811 added pembrolizumab to trastuzumab+chemo for HER2+ + PD-L1 CPS ≥1 — improved ORR + PFS. T-DXd (trastuzumab-deruxtecan) preferred 2L+ for HER2+ post-trastuzumab failure (DESTINY-Gastric01).
