Capabilities

Notes

Risk-stratified archetype. The fundamental treatment split is fit-vs-unfit for intensive 7+3 induction (age, comorbidity, ECOG). Within fit patients, FLT3-ITD/TKD adds midostaurin (RATIFY); within unfit, ven+aza (VIALE-A) is now the 1L standard. APL (PML-RARA) is a separate disease with completely different therapy (ATRA + ATO) — see DIS-APL. STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff before publication. Myeloid expansion (heme) — first myeloid disease end-to-end.
