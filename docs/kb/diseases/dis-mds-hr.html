Capabilities

Notes

Separated from MDS-LR because therapeutic intent (disease modification + transplant bridge) differs from cytopenia management. WHO 5th ed. reclassifies the older "MDS-EB" / "RAEB" terminology to MDS-IB1 (5-9% blasts) and MDS-IB2 (10-19% blasts). TP53-mutated MDS is its own WHO entity with poor HMA + alloHCT outcomes (active research question; no consensus best regimen). STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff before publication. Myeloid expansion (heme).
