Doente
PZ-DIAG-MPN-001 · suspicion lineage: polycythemia_vera
Tissues: bone_marrow, spleen · Hypotheses: DIS-PV, DIS-ET
58M presents with 6-month history of aquagenic pruritus (intense itching after warm shower), facial plethora, occasional headache, blurred vision, and one episode of digital ischemia (transient erythromelalgia) of right index finger. CBC over 3 visits: Hgb 19.2 g/dL (markedly elevated, baseline 14.8 two years prior), Hct 58%, WBC 13.4 (leukocytosis), platelets 562 (mild thrombocytosis), erythrocytosis confirmed. Splenomegaly 16 cm on US. Serum erythropoietin LOW 2.1 mU/mL (suppressed — appropriate for primary erythrocytosis vs elevated in secondary). LDH mildly elevated 245. No tobacco / no chronic hypoxic disease / no testosterone supplementation / no high-altitude exposure. JAK2 V617F testing pending. Strong suspicion of polycythemia vera per WHO 2022 major criteria 1 (Hgb >16.5 M / >16 F) + minor low EPO.
Ensaio combinado: WORKUP-SUSPECTED-MPN-MDS · Очікуваний термін: ~14 дnoв

Etapas de trabalho (25)

  • 1laboratório Contagem completa de sangue com diferencial
    Avaliação da citopenia basal e monitorização contínua da mielossupressão.
  • 2laboratório Sangue Periférico (revisão manual)
    Морфологія еритроцитів, лейкоцитів, тромбоцитів — детекція бластів, dysplasia, schistocytes, abnormal lymphoid forms; обов'язково при будь-якій незрозумілій цитопеnoї або leukocytosis.
  • 3laboratório Contagem de reticulócitos
    Розрізняє hypoproliferative vs hemolytic/blood-loss anemia. Обов'язково при будь-якій непояснеnoй анемії перед extensive workup.
  • 4laboratório Painel Metabólico Completo
    Електроліти (Na, K, Cl, HCO3), креатиnoн + BUN (renal function), глюкоза, кальцій, альбумін, total protein. Базова метаболічна оцінка перед будь-якою терапією + основа TLS risk + dosing adjustments.
  • 5laboratório Testes de Função Hígado
    Avaliar a função hepática — basal + durante os ciclos BR + terapêutica com DAA + monitorização da reactivação do VHB.
  • 6laboratório Lactato Desidrogenase
    A carga tumoral substitui-se; o aumento da LDH sugere transformação ou progressão no linfoma indolente.
  • 7laboratório Estudos de Ferro (Ferritina, TSAT, TIBC, Ferro Sérico)
    Розрізняє iron deficiency vs anemia of chronic disease (ACD) vs hemochromatosis. Обов'язково при анемії неknownго генезу + перед прийняттям рішення про етіологію.
  • 8laboratório Vitamina B12 + Folato
    Megaloblastic anemia workup; macrocytosis (MCV >100 fL) на CBC потребує B12+folate level.
  • 9laboratório ESR + Proteína C-Reactiva
    Markers of systemic inflammation. У гематологічному workup: differential diagnosis запальних vs neoplastic причин лімфаденопатії; ESR > 100 mm/h при myeloma / advanced lymphoma.
  • 10laboratório Painel de coagulação (PT/INR, aPTT, fibrinogénio)
    Pre-biopsy assessment + DIC screen + baseline перед anticoagulant-affected procedures. Обов'язково перед excisional/core biopsy + bone marrow.
  • 11laboratório D-Dimer
    Маркер фібринолізу. При гематологічному workup — DIC suspect (особливо APL), VTE risk, тромботичno ускладнення MPN.
  • 12laboratório Ácido úrico sérico
    Baseline перед cytoreductive therapy + TLS (tumor lysis syndrome) risk assessment. Elevated uric acid + high tumor burden → TLS prophylaxis (allopurinol / rasburicase).
  • 13laboratório Painel de sorologia da hepatite B
    Obrigatória antes de qualquer terapêutica anti-CD20 ou regime contendo rituximab — identificar o risco de reativação do VHB.
  • 14laboratório Anticorpos do VHC (anti-VHC)
    Скриnoнг на HCV. Якщо позитивний → confirm з HCV RNA quantitative (TEST-HCV-RNA). Mandatory для лімфомного workup (HCV-associated MZL, B-cell lymphomas) і перед anti-CD20.
  • 15laboratório Serologia do VIH (HIV-1/2 Ab + p24)
    Mandatory baseline для будь-якого пацієнта з лімфомою (HIV-associated lymphomas — DLBCL, Burkitt, primary CNS), цитопеnoями неknownго генезу. Впливає на treatment intensity + ART continuation.
  • 16imagens Ecocardiografia (ETT) com avaliação da FEVE
    Baseline LVEF + cardiac structure перед anthracycline-containing chemotherapy + alloHSCT planning. Mandatory перед R-CHOP, ABVD, doxorubicin-based regimens.
  • 17laboratório Beta-HCG (Ensaio de gravidez)
    Mandatory screen у childbearing-age жінок перед cytotoxic / teratogenic therapy + перед PET-CT, certain imaging. Більшість chemotherapy схем teratogenic.
  • 18histologia Aspirato de medula óssea (citologia + fluxo + citogenética + molecular)
    Cytologic + immunophenotypic + cytogenetic + molecular characterization. Mandatory у acute leukemia, MDS, MM, MPN. Often combined з trephine (BMAT). Provides material для multiple downstream tests.
  • 19histologia Biopsia da trefina da medula óssea (Core)
    Architecture + cellularity + fibrosis grade (важливо у MPN/PMF) + IHC for staging lymphomas (15-20% NHL з marrow involvement). Component of bone marrow study (зазвичай combined з aspirate).
  • 20histologia Citometria de fluxo (imunofenotipação)
    Immunophenotypic characterization of leukocytes — discriminates lymphoid vs myeloid lineage, B vs T vs NK, mature vs immature, monoclonal vs reactive. Mandatory у acute leukemia + lymphoma + MDS + MPN workup.
  • 21Outros Citogenética convencional (cariótipo)
    Detect chromosomal aberrations: t(9;22) CML, t(15;17) APL, t(8;14) Burkitt, complex karyotype (poor prognosis у MDS/AML/MM). Mandatory у acute leukemia + MDS + MM workup.
  • 22Outros Painel FISH (específico para doenças)
    Detecção direcionada de translocações recorrentes / eliminações. Painel específico da doença: MM (del 17p, t(4;14), t(14;16), ganho 1q, del 1p); AML (PML-RARα, RUNX1-RUNX 1T1, CBFB-MYH11, MLL); CLL (del 11q, Del 13q,del 17P, trissomia 12); MPN (BCR-ABL); linfoma (MYC, BCL2, BCL6 break-apart).
  • 23Outros Molecular alvo: BCR-ABL1 + JAK2 V617F + CALR + MPL
    Diagnostic core для MPN: BCR-ABL1 (CML); JAK2 V617F (PV ~95%, ET ~50%, PMF ~50%); CALR (ET / PMF JAK2-negative); MPL (rare ET / PMF). Послідовnoсть: JAK2 first → if neg, CALR → if neg, MPL.
  • 24Outros Painel mielóide NGS (AML/MDS/MPN)
    Targeted next-generation sequencing for: FLT3 (ITD, TKD), NPM1, IDH1/IDH2, CEBPA, RUNX1, TP53, ASXL1, DNMT3A, TET2, SRSF2, U2AF1, JAK2, CALR, MPL, KIT (D816V). Determines risk stratification + targeted therapy eligibility (FLT3i, IDH1i, IDH2i, midostaurin, etc.).
  • 25histologia Biópsia de tecido + painel IHC + molecular auxiliar
    Trephine критична для MPN diagnosis: megakaryocyte morphology (clustering, atypia) + reticulin/collagen fibrosis grade (MF-0 to MF-3) — основа WHO 2022 differentials між ET, PV, early-PMF, overt-PMF.
    Biopsia preferida: Bone marrow aspirate + trephine biopsy (trephine essential for fibrosis grading у PMF / overlap MPN)
    Início do tratamento com IHC: CD34, CD117, coloração de reticulina, coloração de colágeno (tricroma de Masson), marcadores de megacariócitos (CD41, CD61)

Питання що мають бути закриті (9)

  • MDS vs MPN vs. MDS/MPN sobreposição (CMML, CML atípico, JMML)?
  • Якщо MPN — JAK2/CALR/MPL status визначає subtype + prognostic
  • Якщо MDS — IPSS-R or IPSS-M risk stratification?
  • BCR-ABL1 status — CML excluded?
  • Driver mutation absence (triple-negative MPN ~10%) — додаткові molecular?
  • Citose reativa vs neoplásica (considerar o nível de EPO para exclusão fotovoltaica)?
  • Risco de trombose (MPN) — indicação de anticoagulação?
  • Risk of progression to AML (MDS high-risk, post-MPN AML)?
  • AlloHSCT candidacy (особливо MDS high-risk + PMF intermediate-2 / high)?
