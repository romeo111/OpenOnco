O seu plano oncológico

Diagnosis: Carcinoma endometrial

Versão técnica para o seu médico →

O que foi encontrado

  • DMMR MSH2 germline loss-of-function (dMMR / MSI (microsatellite instability in the tumour)-H) — the system that repairs errors during DNA copying is impaired; often associated with MSI-H, but these are different findings and test results may disagree — your doctor assesses them. Clinical benefit supported by randomised studiesA interpretação requer revisão clínica
  • MSI MSI-H — a high level of changes in short repeated DNA regions in the tumour; it may help your doctor select immunotherapy, but does not guarantee a treatment response. Clinical benefit supported by randomised studiesA interpretação requer revisão clínica
