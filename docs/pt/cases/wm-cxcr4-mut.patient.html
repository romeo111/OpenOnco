O que isto significa para o tratamento

A) Standard option

Carfilzomib + Rituximab + Dexamethasone (CaRD) × 6 cycles — for CXCR4-WHIM-mutated WM (BTKi-resistant subset)

Duration (original source text): 6 cycles induction; rituximab maintenance optional based on response; cycle — 28 days

Expected tolerability class:

Moderate to high burden

This regimen is closer to intensive treatment and usually requires careful monitoring. This is an approximate class for the whole regimen, not a prediction for you personally: tolerability depends on your health, doses and supportive treatment.

Awaiting review by doctors

Carfilzomib

blocks cellular waste disposal; a treatment foundation for multiple myeloma (bortezomib, carfilzomib)

Medicine form: intravenous infusion (a drip) at the clinic.

NHSU covers this medicine, but not for this diagnosis

Rituximab

an antibody that finds B cells and signals the immune system to destroy them, including lymphoma cells. Вводиться внутрішньовенно або підшкірно у складі схем для лімфом і CLL.

B) Alternative option

Zanubrutinib em monoterapia contínua para a MW r/r (esquema ASPEN) — alternativa ao ibrutinib

Duration (original source text): Continuous; cycle — 28 days

Low to moderate burden
