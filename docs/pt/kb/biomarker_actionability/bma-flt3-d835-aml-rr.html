Resumo das provasFLT3-D835 in R/R AML: gilteritinib superior to salvage chemo (ADMIRAL, Perl 2019 — pre-specified subset analysis showed activity across both ITD and D835/TKD; D835 subset response rates similar to ITD overall). Gilteritinib is preferred type-I FLT3i for D835-mutant R/R disease. Quizartinib remains contraindicated (no D835 activity).
