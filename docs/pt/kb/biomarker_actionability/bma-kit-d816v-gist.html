Resumo das provasKIT D816V in GIST is rare as primary mutation (more typical of systemic mastocytosis) but emerges as a secondary resistance mutation under imatinib pressure. Confers imatinib + sunitinib resistance. Ripretinib (INVICTUS, Blay Lancet Oncol 2020 — switch- control inhibitor with broad activation-loop coverage) and avapritinib (NAVIGATOR; FDA-approved in PDGFRA D842V and ASM) retain activity vs D816V.

Notas
