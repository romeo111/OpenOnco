Resumo das provasMLH1 germline loss-of-function in ovarian produces dMMR/MSI-H phenotype. Pan- tumor MSI-H ICI eligibility (pembrolizumab per KEYNOTE-158, dostarlimab per GARNET) supersedes tumor-specific lines via tissue-agnostic FDA approval. MSI-H is rare (~3%) in EOC; pembrolizumab tissue-agnostic applies in pretreated setting.

Notas

Germline MLH1 loss → cascade testing mandatory (Lynch syndrome). Family meets Amsterdam/Bethesda criteria. Pan-tumor MSI-H ICI eligibility supersedes tumor- specific lines.
