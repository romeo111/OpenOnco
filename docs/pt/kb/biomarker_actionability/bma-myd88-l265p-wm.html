Resumo das provasMYD88 L265P present in >90% of WM. Activates NF-κB via BTK/IRAK signaling — rationale for BTKi. Ibrutinib monotherapy (iNNOVATE substudy 2 / arm A — Treon et al. NEJM 2015 R/R; iNNOVATE — Dimopoulos et al. NEJM 2018 1L+R/R with rituximab) and zanubrutinib (ASPEN — Tam et al. Blood 2020) FDA-approved. CXCR4 WHIM-like co-mutation reduces but does not abolish response.
