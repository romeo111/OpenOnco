Resumo das provasPD-L1 Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) is the primary predictive biomarker for pembrolizumab selection in metastatic NSCLC without driver alterations. Three threshold-stratified eligibility bands: TPS ≥50% — pembrolizumab monotherapy 1L preferred (KEYNOTE-024; mPFS 10.3 vs 6.0 mo, HR 0.50); TPS ≥1% — pembrolizumab + carboplatin + pemetrexed (non-sq, KEYNOTE-189) or pembrolizumab + carboplatin + paclitaxel/nab-paclitaxel (sq, KEYNOTE-407); TPS 1-49% — chemo-IO combination preferred over pembro mono. Testing by IHC 22C3 pharmDx mandatory on FFPE specimen. Threshold-gated indication selection is performed by the algorithm layer (ALGO-NSCLC, IND-NSCLC-PDL1-HIGH-MET-1L, IND-NSCLC-PDL1-LOW-NONSQ-MET-1L); this BMA entry surfaces ESCAT tier context only.

Notas

Variant_qualifier null — TPS is a continuous score (0-100%); threshold gating enforced by the algorithm/indication layer. TPS ≠ CPS: TPS counts only PD-L1+ tumor cells; CPS additionally counts PD-L1+ immune cells. EGFR/ALK/ROS1/BRAF/KRAS/MET driver alterations take priority over PD-L1 TPS — algorithm checks driver status first.
