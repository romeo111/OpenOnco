Notas

R273 is among the top three most-mutated TP53 codons (with R175 and R248). DNA-contact class: R273 directly contacts the DNA major groove via its guanidinium side chain. R→H substitution preserves the thermal stability of the protein fold (distinguishing it from structural R175H) but eliminates specific DNA binding. R273H is one of the best-characterized GOF p53 mutants — promotes chemoresistance via NF-κB activation, drives invasion/metastasis through CXCR4 and RhoA pathways, reprograms metabolism (mevalonate pathway upregulation). Clinical implications mirror the TP53-mutant class: AML/MDS — venetoclax-azacitidine + alloSCT consideration; CLL/MCL — contraindicates chemoimmuno; MM — high-risk cytogenetics composite routes to quadruplet. Notably, R273H tumors are particularly resistant to platinum chemotherapy across solid-tumor types per multiple correlative analyses — relevant for ovarian, lung, esophageal cancer treatment selection.
