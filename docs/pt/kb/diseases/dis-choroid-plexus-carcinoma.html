Notas

One of the strongest single-tumor signals for germline TP53 — CPC diagnosis in a child should trigger germline TP53 testing regardless of family history (per Chompret + Li-Fraumeni 2015 testing criteria). Treatment: maximal safe surgical resection (gross total resection is prognostic dominant — bleeding-prone tumor with intraventricular drainage challenges); adjuvant chemotherapy (regimens vary — ICE, carboplatin-VP16, CCNU-based); craniospinal RT in age-appropriate patients (typically deferred under age 3 to avoid neurocognitive toll — high-dose chemo + autoSCT bridge sometimes used). Li-Fraumeni carriers require lifelong Toronto-protocol surveillance after treatment (cross-syndrome second-primary risk).
