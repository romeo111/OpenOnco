Capacidades

Notas

CLL = bone marrow + blood disease (≥5K monoclonal B-cells); SLL = same biology, lymph-node-predominant. Treated identically. iwCLL criteria for treatment indication: progressive cytopenia, symptomatic splenomegaly/lymphadenopathy, B-symptoms, autoimmune cytopenia not responsive to steroids, lymphocyte doubling time <6 mo. Asymptomatic CLL = active surveillance regardless of stage.
