Notas

Pediatric cystic nephroma is a strong DICER1-syndrome signal — diagnosis in any child should trigger DICER1 germline testing AND cascade-testing of parents + siblings. Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome is a separate (non- DICER1) etiology that shares the Wilms-spectrum abdominal-tumour surveillance protocol (abdominal US q3-4 months from birth to age 7; AFP q2-3 months from birth to age 4 — focused on hepatoblastoma). Treatment: maximal safe surgical resection (nephron-sparing partial nephrectomy when anatomically feasible; total nephrectomy if not). Histopathology after resection is critical — distinguishes pure cystic nephroma (benign-borderline, surveillance only) from cystic partially differentiated nephroblastoma (Wilms-spectrum, requires adjuvant chemo per stage) and from progression to anaplastic sarcoma of the kidney (high-grade malignant, requires multimodal therapy). Lifelong DICER1-syndrome surveillance for germline-positive survivors: chest imaging (PPB risk), abdominal-pelvic US (ovarian, Sertoli-Leydig risk in girls), thyroid US, and CNS imaging (pineoblastoma, pituitary blastoma risk under age 5).
