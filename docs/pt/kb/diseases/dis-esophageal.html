Capacidades

Notas

Scaffolded for Phase GI-1: 1 RF (high-grade dysphagia / aspiration — most common acute presentation), 1 indication (CROSS-style scaffold noting RT not modellable), 1 algorithm. Full histology-stratified algorithm (squamous: definitive CRT for unresectable, CROSS for resectable; adeno: FLOT preferred for resectable, CROSS alternative) + adjuvant nivolumab post-CROSS without pCR + metastatic biomarker- stratified ICI/chemo combos await ratification + clinical authoring. Histology branching is critical — squamous and adeno have largely parallel but partially divergent paradigms. Per-prompt allowance: one disease YAML covers both, with histology field on Indication applicability.
