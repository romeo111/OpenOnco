Notas

Risk-stratified by FLIPI / FLIPI2 (informational); 1L decision driven more by GELF criteria (high vs low tumor burden) than FLIPI score. FL grade 3B treated as DLBCL — separate Indication (IND-DLBCL-1L-RCHOP). Transformation to DLBCL (Richter-like, but FL→DLBCL) re-enters DLBCL pathway with worse prognosis baseline.
