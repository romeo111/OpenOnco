Capacidades

Notas

Mandatory FISH break-apart for MYC + BCL2 + BCL6 on every newly- diagnosed DLBCL — without it, double-hit is missed. Distinct from Burkitt (Ki67 ~100%, BCL2−) by Ki67 typically 70-90% + BCL2+.
