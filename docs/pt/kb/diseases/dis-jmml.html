Notas

JMML is a rare pediatric overlap myeloid neoplasm with myelodysplastic and myeloproliferative features. Allogeneic HCT is the only curative treatment (5-yr EFS ~50%). The RAS/MAPK pathway is activated in ~90% of cases; PTPN11 mutations (SHP2 gain-of-function) are the most common driver. SHP2 inhibitors (RMC-4630, TNO155, SHP099, ERAS-601) are in phase I/II trials, including pediatric expansion cohorts. Important: germline PTPN11 (Noonan syndrome) is associated with a usually self-limiting myeloproliferative process that may not require HCT — distinguishing somatic vs germline PTPN11 is clinically critical. Somatic PTPN11 mutations confer worse prognosis. Azacitidine is used as bridge to HCT. Post-HCT relapse treated with second transplant or investigational agents.
