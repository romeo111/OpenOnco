Notas

LAM is a rare, progressive cystic lung disease caused by TSC1/TSC2 loss-of-function and consequent mTORC1 hyperactivation. Sirolimus (rapamycin) is FDA/EMA-approved for LAM (MILES trial, 2011: improved FEV1 trajectory, reduced VEGF-D). Everolimus is used for TSC-related manifestations including SEGA and renal AML. Disease is estrogen-sensitive: exacerbations during pregnancy, use of hormonal contraceptives discouraged. Diagnosis: high-resolution CT (diffuse bilateral thin-walled cysts) ± serum VEGF-D ≥800 pg/mL (diagnostic without biopsy in right clinical context). LAM Foundation guidelines (NHLBI/ERS) primary reference; NCCN does not cover LAM.
