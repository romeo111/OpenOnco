Capacidades

Notas

Risk-stratified archetype split into TWO entities (DIS-MDS-LR and DIS-MDS-HR) because therapeutic intent is fundamentally different: cytopenia management vs disease modification + transplant. IPSS-R remains most widely used; IPSS-M (2022) integrates molecular features and reclassifies ~46%. del(5q) carries unique lenalidomide indication. RS-MDS (SF3B1+) is the luspatercept population. STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff before publication. Myeloid expansion (heme).
