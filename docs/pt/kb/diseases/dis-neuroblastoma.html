Notas

COG + SIOPEN risk-stratified treatment frameworks. Anti-GD2 immuno- therapy (dinutuximab + cytokines) is high-risk standard since 2009; ch14.18/Naxitamab as alternative. Lorlatinib + crizotinib for ALK- mutant subset (cooperative-group trials). MIBG-iodine131 + lutathera therapy for MIBG-avid relapsed disease. Surveillance for syndromic predisposition carriers: abdominal US + urinary catecholamines q3-6mo to age 8 in Costello + Beckwith-Wiedemann.
