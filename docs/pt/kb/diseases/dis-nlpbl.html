Notas

WHO 5th-ed renamed NLPHL → NLPBL (now classified as B-cell, not Hodgkin). CD20+ → rituximab-responsive. Early-stage: ISRT alone curative in many. Advanced: R-CHOP-like (rituximab + chemo, reusing DLBCL pathway) preferred over ABVD.
