Notas

Scaffolded for batch #2: focus on advanced-stage (FIGO III-IV) HGSC 1L systemic + maintenance. 4 RFs (debulking-status MDT-trigger, HRD-actionability, frailty/age, perioperative VTE risk). Regimens: carbo+pacli ± bev backbone, olaparib maintenance HRD+, niraparib all-comer maintenance, bev maintenance per PAOLA-1. Out-of-scope this commit: rare histologies (clear-cell, endometrioid, mucinous, low-grade serous have distinct paradigms); recurrent platinum-sensitive vs platinum-resistant 2L+ algorithms; borderline tumors. HRD testing (BIO-HRD-STATUS) is the central biomarker — defines PARPi-maintenance benefit. BRCA1/2 germline + somatic via reflex testing.
