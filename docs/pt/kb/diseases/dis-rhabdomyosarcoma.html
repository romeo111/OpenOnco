Notas

PAX3-FOXO1 fusion status is the strongest single prognostic marker. EpSSG (European) and COG (North American) cooperatives study risk- adapted regimens. Fusion-positive ARMS now receives intensified treatment; fusion-negative ARMS behaves more like ERMS. Surveillance in Costello + LFS + DICER1 carriers: abdominal/pelvic US q3-6 mo to age 8-10; symptom-driven imaging for soft-tissue mass at any age.
