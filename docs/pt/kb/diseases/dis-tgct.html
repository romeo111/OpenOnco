Notas

TGCT is classified as locally aggressive (diffuse-type) or benign (localized). Despite benign histology, diffuse-type TGCT (formerly PVNS) causes severe joint destruction, pain, and functional disability, with high surgical recurrence rates (25–50%). Pexidartinib (CSF1R inhibitor) is the only FDA-approved systemic therapy (2019, ILLUMINATE trial): ORR 38%, durable response in responders, but significant hepatotoxicity risk (REMS program; transaminase monitoring mandatory). Surgery remains the primary modality; pexidartinib is reserved for patients with severe morbidity not amenable to surgery improvement. Malignant TGCT (9252/3) is extremely rare.
