ClasseConjugado anticorpos-médicos anti-CD30 (carga útil MMAE)
MecanismoIgG1 com o objetivo CD30 conjugado com MMAE através de linker clivável — internalizado por células CD30+, libera MMAE intracelularmente → interrupção de microtubulos → apoptose.
Dose típicaALCL/PTCL CD30+ 1L (CHP-Bv): 1.8 mg/kg IV day 1 of 21-day cycle × 6. Hodgkin 1L (A+AVD): 1.2 mg/kg IV days 1+15 of 28-day cycle × 6. Mycosis fungoides r/r (CD30+ ≥10%): 1.8 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks.

Avisos

  • Leucoencefalopatia multifocal progressiva (LMP, rara)

Notas

Used in: 1L T-cell lymphomas with CD30+ (ALCL universal CD30+; ~40-60% PTCL NOS / AITL CD30+) — ECHELON-2 trial showed CHP-Bv superior to CHOP. 1L Hodgkin (A+AVD = ABVD with brentuximab replacing bleomycin — ECHELON-1; bleomycin removed due to lung toxicity additive risk). R/R cutaneous lymphomas (MF/Sézary CD30+, primary cutaneous ALCL). NEVER combine with bleomycin. Ukraine: registered, NOT НСЗУ-reimbursed.
