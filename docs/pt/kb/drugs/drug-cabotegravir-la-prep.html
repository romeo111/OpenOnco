ClasseInibidor da transferência de fio de integração do VIH-1 (INSTI) — injectável de longa duração para a PrEP
MecanismoSecond-generation HIV-1 integrase strand-transfer inhibitor (INSTI) formulated as a long-acting injectable nanosuspension. Blocks integration of viral DNA into the host chromosome — same INSTI mechanism as dolutegravir and bictegravir, with similar high barrier to resistance. Sustained-release IM depot provides therapeutic plasma cabotegravir for ~2-3 months per dose. As HIV-1 PRE-EXPOSURE PROPHYLAXIS in HIV-negative adults at substantial risk, CAB-LA reduces sexual HIV acquisition by ~69% vs daily oral FTC/TDF in HPTN 083 (cisgender MSM / transgender women) and by ~89% in HPTN 084 (cisgender women at risk in sub-Saharan Africa) — superior efficacy to oral PrEP, driven by improved adherence over daily pills. Dose típicaAdult / adolescent (≥35 kg) PrEP: Loading: 600 mg IM in gluteal muscle on month 0 and month 1 (~4 weeks apart). Maintenance: 600 mg IM in gluteal muscle every 2 months (8 weeks). Optional oral lead-in (HISTORICAL — REMOVED from FDA label 2023): Cabotegravir 30 mg PO once daily × 4 weeks before first injection to confirm tolerability. Per FDA 2023 label update and HPTN data, this oral lead-in is NO LONGER REQUIRED — direct IM injection is now standard. APRETUDE (CAB-LA for PrEP) is the first long-acting injectable HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis option (FDA 2021). Bimonthly gluteal IM injection; per FDA 2023 label update, no oral lead-in required. SUPERIOR to oral FTC/TDF PrEP in head-to-head trials (HPTN 083: ~69% lower HIV incidence among MSM/TGW; HPTN 084: ~89% lower among cisgender African women) — driven by adherence advantage. Cancer-prevention rationale: preventing HIV acquisition prevents downstream AIDS-defining cancers (Kaposi sarcoma, NHL incl. PCNSL and Burkitt, invasive cervical cancer) and several non-AIDS-defining cancers (HPV-driven anal, oropharyngeal, hepatocellular, Hodgkin lymphoma).
