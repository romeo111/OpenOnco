Fatos sobre as drogas

ClasseConjugado de anticorpos-médicos com TROP2 (carga útil de deruxtecano/inibidor do topo-I)
MecanismoHumanized anti-TROP2 IgG1 monoclonal antibody linked to deruxtecan (DXd, exatecan-derivative topoisomerase-I inhibitor) via cleavable tetrapeptide linker. Drug-to-antibody ratio ~4:1, bystander effect on neighboring TROP2-low cells. TROP2 is broadly expressed on epithelial tumors (breast, NSCLC).
Dose típica6 mg/kg IV q3 semanas até a progressão da doença ou toxicidade inaceitável. Premedicação com antieméticos + corticosteróides + lubrificação ocular recomendada.

Notas

TROPION-Breast01 (NCT05104866): Phase III, HR+/HER2- pre-treated metastatic breast cancer. Dato-DXd vs investigator-choice chemo. Median PFS 6.9 vs 4.9 mo (HR 0.63). FDA approval Jan 2025. TROPION-Lung01 (NCT04656652): Phase III, pre-treated advanced NSCLC. Modest PFS benefit overall; greater benefit in non-squamous subset and in actionable-genomic-alteration NSCLC. Stomatitis + ocular toxicity = distinguishing AE profile vs T-DXd. Trial-source SRCs for TROPION-Breast01 and TROPION-Lung01 not yet ingested into KB — FLAG for follow-up.
