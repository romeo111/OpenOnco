Avisos

  • Reactivação do vírus da hepatite B
  • Leucoencefalopatia multifocal progressiva

Notas

Alternative anti-CD20 for patients with rituximab intolerance, or in CLL/follicular lymphoma settings. Less established for MZL than rituximab; data extrapolated from CLL/FL trials.
