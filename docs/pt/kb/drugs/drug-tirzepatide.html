Fatos sobre as drogas

ClasseDual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist (once-weekly)
MecanismoSynthetic 39-amino-acid peptide that activates both glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and GLP-1 receptors. Dual agonism produces greater glycemic control and weight loss than GLP-1 monoagonism. At obesity dose (max 15 mg weekly), SURMOUNT-1 trial demonstrated ~21% mean weight loss vs ~3% on placebo — superior to semaglutide 2.4 mg. Obesity is associated with 13+ cancers (IARC/WCRF) — sustained weight loss is a cancer-prevention intervention.
Dose típicaSC: aumento da dose durante ≥20 semanas para maximizar a tolerância: Semana 1-4: 2,5 mg SC semanal Semana 5-8: 5 mg SC settimanal Semana 9-12: 7,5 mg SC Semanal 13-16: 10 mg SC semanas Semanal 17-20: 12,5 mg SC hebdomadaire Semana 21 em diante: 15 mg SC quinzenal (dose máxima de obesidade) Injectar SC abdome/coxa/braço superior qualquer dia da semana; rotar locais. doses de manutenção mais baixas (5, 10, 15 mg) aceitáveis com base na resposta/tolerância.
