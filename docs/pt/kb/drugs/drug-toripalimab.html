Fatos sobre as drogas

ClasseAnti-PD-1 humanizado anticorpo monoclonal IgG4 (inibidor do ponto de controlo imune)
MecanismoAnticorpo monoclonal IgG4 humanizado ligando o receptor de morte celular programada 1 (PD-1) às células T ativadas, bloqueando interações PD-1/PD-L1 e PD-1PD- L2 e restaurando a atividade antitumoral das células T citotóxicas.A mesma classe mecânica anti-PD-1 geral como pembrolizumab (DRUG-PEMBROLIZUMAB) e nivolumab ( DRUG-NIVOLUMAB). Estudada especificamente em carcinoma nasofaríngeo associado ao VEB (NPC), um tipo tumoral com um microambiente imune claramente inflamado, impulsionado pelo VEB-antigénio.
Dose típicaPer US FDA label (Loqtorzi) and the JUPITER-02 trial protocol: toripalimab 240 mg IV every 3 weeks, combined with gemcitabine + cisplatin (GP) for up to 6 cycles, followed by toripalimab monotherapy (240 mg IV q3w, or 480 mg IV q6w per label) until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or up to 2 years total. Notas

STUB — draft entity pending Clinical Co-Lead sign-off (CHARTER §6.1; currently dev-mode-exempted). Anti-PD-1 backbone of NPC metastatic 1L combination per JUPITER-02 (Mai HQ et al., Nat Med 2021): toripalimab + gemcitabine + cisplatin significantly improved PFS/ORR vs chemo alone in treatment-naive recurrent/metastatic NPC, leading to FDA approval (Loqtorzi) Oct 2023. Reuse this entity rather than duplicate. Distinct from camrelizumab (DRUG-CAMRELIZUMAB), a separate anti-PD-1 antibody studied in CAPTAIN-1st for the same indication — not interchangeable identities despite similar mechanism/regimen role.
