Notas

ESCAT IA. HER2CLIMB established tucatinib + trastuzumab + capecitabine for HER2+ metastatic ≥3L (post-trastuzumab + post-T-DXd in current era). Major unmet need addressed: active brain metastases — first regimen with prospectively-demonstrated intracranial activity. CNS-PFS doubling justifies preferred positioning when brain mets present (active or treated). MRI brain required pre-treatment to characterize CNS disease. Diarrhea management dominant clinical workflow burden — combined effect of tucatinib + capecitabine. UA access barrier: tucatinib not registered; named-patient import / EAP only. T-DM1 remains an alternative when tucatinib not accessible (KATHERINE / EMILIA data — not modeled in this batch).
