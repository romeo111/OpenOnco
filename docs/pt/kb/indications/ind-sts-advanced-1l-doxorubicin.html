Notas

MDT at specialized sarcoma center is strongly recommended before starting chemotherapy — histological subtype significantly affects regimen selection. Key subtype-specific alternatives to consider at MDT: - Leiomyosarcoma: gemcitabine+docetaxel has specific activity; may be preferred 2L - Myxoid liposarcoma: trabectedin particularly effective (YONDELIS) - Synovial sarcoma: ifosfamide-sensitive — AI preferred over dox alone at some centers - WD/DD liposarcoma: doxorubicin active; CDK4/MDM2 targeted therapy in trials - ASPS (alveolar soft part sarcoma): anti-VEGFR agents more active than anthracyclines
