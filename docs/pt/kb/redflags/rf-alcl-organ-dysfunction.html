DefiniçãoBaseline organ dysfunction precluding standard CHOP/CHP-Bv doses in ALCL: LVEF <50% (anthracycline / brentuximab cardiotoxicity overlap), CrCl <30 (cyclophosphamide dose constraints), bilirubin >3× ULN (vincristine / brentuximab metabolism — brentuximab dose-modify Child-Pugh B; avoid C), or pre-existing peripheral neuropathy ≥grade 2 (vincristine + brentuximab both neuropathic — combination prohibitive).

Notas

ALCL is overwhelmingly CD30+, so CHP-Bv (ECHELON-2) is the standard backbone — but brentuximab adds neuropathy on top of vincristine. With baseline neuropathy ≥2, omit vincristine (CHP without V) or substitute gemcitabine-based salvage. LVEF <50%: substitute liposomal doxorubicin or omit anthracycline (GEM-P). Severe hepatic dysfunction: brentuximab contraindicated in Child-Pugh C.
