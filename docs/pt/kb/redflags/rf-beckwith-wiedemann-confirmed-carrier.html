Origem da bandeira vermelha

DefiniçãoPatient has a confirmed Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome (BWS) molecular diagnosis — pathogenic alteration at the 11p15.5 imprinting locus (CDKN1C, H19, KCNQ1OT1, IGF2). BWS confers an estimated 5-10% lifetime embryonal tumor risk during infancy and early childhood (especially Wilms tumor with IC1/H19 hypermethylation subtype, hepatoblastoma across subtypes). The risk window is narrow — most tumors arise before age 8 — so intensive prevention-time surveillance is critical. Prevention-persona confirmed-carrier RedFlag (§20).
