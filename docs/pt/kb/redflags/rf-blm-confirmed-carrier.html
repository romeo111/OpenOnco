Origem da bandeira vermelha

DefiniçãoConfirmed germline pathogenic / likely-pathogenic variant in the BLM gene — Bloom syndrome carrier status. Biallelic BLM inactivation causes Bloom syndrome (BS) — autosomal-recessive chromosomal- instability disorder with severe growth restriction, photosensitive facial erythema, immunodeficiency, and dramatically elevated risk of a broad spectrum of cancers (CRC, leukemia, lymphoma, breast, skin) with median age of cancer diagnosis ~25-30 years. Heterozygous (carrier) status — the focus of this RedFlag — confers a modest signal for colorectal cancer (RR ~1.5-2.0) and possibly breast cancer (RR ~1.5; evidence less consistent).
