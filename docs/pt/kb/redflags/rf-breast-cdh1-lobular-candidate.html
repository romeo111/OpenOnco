Origem da bandeira vermelha

DefiniçãoGermline CDH1 (E-cadherin) pathogenic variant in invasive lobular breast carcinoma — INFORMATIONAL signal. CDH1 is the hereditary diffuse gastric cancer (HDGC) gene; carriers face ~40-50% lifetime invasive lobular breast cancer risk and ~70% diffuse gastric cancer risk. No PARPi indication and no breast-cancer-specific systemic therapy decision keyed on CDH1 status. Surfaces in MDT brief to trigger: (1) cascade testing of first-degree relatives, (2) prophylactic total gastrectomy discussion (HDGC management standard per NCCN hereditary GI), (3) enhanced breast surveillance vs risk-reducing mastectomy counseling. Lobular subtype itself influences endocrine-therapy selection (CDK4/6i + AI typical; some chemotherapy regimens less effective in lobular vs ductal), but that is subtype-driven, not CDH1-genotype-driven.
