Origem da bandeira vermelha

DefiniçãoConfirmed germline pathogenic / likely-pathogenic monoallelic variant in CHEK2. Most common population variant is c.1100delC truncating loss-of-function (~1.5% European population frequency); other pathogenic variants include c.444+1G>A (splice), p.Ile157Thr (missense with modest effect), and large deletions. Heterozygous CHEK2 carriers have moderate-penetrance breast cancer risk (RR ~2.0-3.0; lifetime ~20-30% for truncating variants, lower for missense), modest CRC risk (RR ~1.5-2.0; lifetime ~5-10% over baseline), prostate cancer (RR ~2-3 with high-grade enrichment for c.1100delC), thyroid cancer (smaller signal). Most common moderate-penetrance variant in multi- gene panels among European-ancestry cohorts. Notas

Prevenção-persona RedFlag para portadores heterozigotos CHEK2. Estimativas de risco variam substancialmente de acordo com a variante: truncar > erva > erro. Rotas motoras para a vigilância mamária aprimorada (mamografia anual a partir da idade 40 + consideração de RM), rastreamento de CRC modestamente apertado (colonoscopia a partir de 40 anos com intervalo q5y por NCCN), e consciência da próstata a partir dos 40-45 anos para os homens com c. 1100delC.
