Origem da bandeira vermelha

DefiniçãoFrailty/age profile precluding doublet-intensive or triplet chemo: ECOG ≥3, OR (age ≥80 + Charlson ≥3), OR composite (age ≥75 + albumin <3.0 + ≥2 comorbidities). Triggers de-escalation toward 5-FU/LV mono, capecitabine mono, or best supportive care.

Notas

IDEA pooled analysis: 3 mo CAPOX adjuvant non-inferior to 6 mo for low-risk (T1-3 N1) stage III — explicitly designed for elderly / fragile. FOCUS-2 trial: capecitabine ± oxaliplatin in elderly mCRC showed acceptable efficacy with reduced toxicity. BSC alone if PS 4 or organ failure precludes any chemo.
