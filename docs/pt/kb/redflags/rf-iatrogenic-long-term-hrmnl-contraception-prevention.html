Origem da bandeira vermelha

DefiniçãoCumulative use of estrogen-containing or combined hormonal contraception (combined oral contraceptive, combined patch, combined vaginal ring) ≥10 years — IARC Group 1 carcinogen (estrogen-progestogen combined oral contraceptives). Modest, small-magnitude relative-risk elevation: breast cancer +1.2-1.3x during current/recent use (effect attenuates within 10 years of stopping; population-level absolute increment ~13 extra cases per 100,000 woman-years for ages 35-39); cervical cancer +1.5-2.0x at ≥5y use among high-risk HPV-positive cohort (mechanism: probable co-factor for HPV persistence + cervical squamous-cell carcinoma progression). NOTE: this iatrogenic-persona RF is intentionally distinct from RF-REPRODUCTIVE-OCP-LONG-TERM, which carries the BALANCED risk-benefit counseling framing (substantial ovarian + endometrial protection lifelong). Notas
