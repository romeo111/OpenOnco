Larynx- preservation-candidacy gate for locally advanced laryngeal SCC — routes ALGO-LARYNGEAL-1L step 5 to primary total laryngectomy instead of RTOG 91-11 concurrent chemoradiation. Grounded in the Disease-level narrative's "patient preference/fitness drives choice" framing plus well-established organ-preservation-candidacy criteria (RTOG 91-11 itself excluded T4 disease with cartilage destruction / >1 cm tongue-base invasion from its organ-preservation comparison). Distinct from RF-HNSCC-ORGAN-DYSFUNCTION (reused unchanged at ALGO-LARYNGEAL-1L step 6), which gates cisplatin FITNESS rather than larynx-preservation CANDIDACY. clinical_direction set to `intensify` (not `investigate`) per independent safety-review fix: this RF escalates from organ-preservation CRT to primary radical surgery when it fires, and every other algorithm-shifting RedFlag in this KB uses intensify/de-escalate/hold, never investigate (confirmed by grepping redflags/ sibling files) — the loader's _check_redflag_contracts flags investigate+non-empty shifts_algorithm as a re-classification warning.
