Origem da bandeira vermelha

DefiniçãoHigh habitual intake of sugar-sweetened beverages (SSBs) — sodas, fruit drinks, sports drinks, sweetened teas/coffees — ≥1 serving (~250 mL) per day. Associated with increased risk of obesity-related cancers including colorectal cancer (RR ~1.2-1.3 in women with ≥2 servings/ day per Nurses' Health Study II; Hur et al. Gut 2021), pancreatic cancer (modest signal in some cohorts), early-onset CRC (under age 50 — Hur 2021 reported ~RR 2.2 for ≥2 servings/day vs <1/wk), and endometrial + post-menopausal breast cancer via obesity pathway. Mechanism: rapid glycemic load → insulin/IGF-1 axis activation + weight gain + adiposity. SSBs are the largest single dietary source of added sugar in many populations. Risk modifiable via substitution with water, unsweetened beverages, low-calorie sweeteners (though recent evidence raises questions about non-nutritive sweetener safety — see controversies)....
