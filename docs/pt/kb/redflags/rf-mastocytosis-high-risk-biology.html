Notas

KIT D816V is present in ≥90% of advSM and is the actionable target for midostaurin (RATIFY/PATHWAY studies) and avapritinib (PATHFINDER, EXPLORER). AHN component (most often myeloid neoplasm — CMML, MDS, MPN-U) is treated separately per its own algorithm AND with KIT-directed therapy. Direction INTENSIFY → KIT-targeted therapy + AHN-directed regimen. STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff.
