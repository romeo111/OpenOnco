Notas

WHO 5th ed. (2022/2023) C-findings define advSM and trigger cytoreductive therapy (avapritinib, midostaurin, cladribine, IFN-α). Per NCCN-SM: presence of any C-finding mandates KIT-directed therapy regardless of symptomatic load. Direction INTENSIFY shifts toward avapritinib (KIT D816V-positive ASM/SM-AHN/MCL) per PATHFINDER trial. STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff.
