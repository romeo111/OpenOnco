Notas

Triggers de-escalation in MDS-HR (skip alloHCT track; consider lower HMA dose or supportive care only) and in MDS-LR (avoid lenalidomide if severe renal impairment; avoid ESA if uncontrolled HTN). CrCl <30 also drives lenalidomide dose reduction + careful HMA dosing. STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff.
