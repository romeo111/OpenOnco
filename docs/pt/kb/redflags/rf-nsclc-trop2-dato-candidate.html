Origem da bandeira vermelha

DefiniçãoTROP2 expression in NSCLC — datopotamab deruxtecan (Dato-DXd) is a TROP2-directed deruxtecan antibody-drug conjugate. TROPION-Lung01 (Ahn 2023) phase-3 randomized previously-treated metastatic NSCLC to Dato-DXd vs docetaxel (mPFS 4.4 vs 3.7 mo, HR 0.75; benefit concentrated in non-squamous subgroup mPFS 5.6 vs 3.7 mo, HR 0.63). TROP2 is broadly expressed across NSCLC histologies (IHC enrollment not required) — TROP2 IHC is informational, not gating. Candidate RF positions Dato-DXd in 2L+ non-squamous NSCLC as a non-platinum cytotoxic alternative to docetaxel.

Notas

Candidate RF — TROPION-Lung01 trial-Source not yet ingested (SRC-TROPION-LUNG01-AHN-2024 flag for ingestion chunk). TROP2 IHC is informational, NOT a gating biomarker — pivotal trial enrolled unselected populations. Histology-based gate (non-squamous) reflects the subgroup analysis showing benefit concentration. clinical_direction = investigate because absolute PFS benefit modest (~0.7 mo overall; ~1.9 mo non-squamous) and OS not reported significantly improved at primary analysis. Excludes patients with actionable drivers (EGFR/ALK/ROS1/BRAF/MET/RET/NTRK/KRAS-G12C/HER2-mut) — those have driver-targeted 2L. ILD class warning per deruxtecan platform — pneumonitis ~3-9% all-grade. Promote to draft:false after trial-Source ingestion + clearer FDA label decision (FDA AdComm 2024-2025 outcomes pending). Note: TROPION-Lung02 (Dato-DXd + pembro 1L) separate trial; out of scope here.
