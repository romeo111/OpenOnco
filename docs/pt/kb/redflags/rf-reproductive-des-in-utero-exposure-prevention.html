Origem da bandeira vermelha

DefiniçãoIn-utero exposure to diethylstilbestrol (DES) — synthetic non-steroidal estrogen prescribed 1940-1971 (USA) and 1948-1978 (Europe) to women with prior miscarriage or threatened miscarriage. DES-exposed daughters carry ~40-fold increased risk of clear-cell adenocarcinoma of the vagina and cervix (peak incidence ages 15-30 but cases continue into older ages), ~2-fold lifetime risk of breast cancer (post-menopausal incidence doubles after age ~40), and structural reproductive anomalies (T-shaped uterus, vaginal adenosis, cervical hood). DES-exposed mothers themselves have ~30% increased breast cancer risk. Although the exposed cohort is aging out (most exposures pre-1978), surveillance continues lifelong. Prevention-persona RedFlag (§20 v0.3 reproductive expansion). Cohort still in active surveillance pathway through ~2050 given peak prescription years.

Notas

Prevenção-persona RedFlag para a coorte de exposição in-utero do DES. O controle é intensificado em relação à população geral (pélvico anual com cuidadosa inspeção vaginal/cervical; colposcopia se citologia anormal; mamografia no limiar de idade mais jovem por risco de modificador NCCN; considerar o rastreamento mamario RM adjuvante com risco duplicado).
