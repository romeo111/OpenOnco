Origem da bandeira vermelha

DefiniçãoLong-term combined oral contraceptive use (≥10 cumulative years). Mixed cancer risk profile per IARC Monograph 100A + Collaborative Group on Hormonal Factors in Breast Cancer + IARC reviews: - Modest BREAST cancer risk increase during current use + ~5y post- cessation (RR ~1.08-1.20; effect declines after cessation, baseline risk restored ~10y post-cessation). - Modest CERVICAL cancer risk increase with long-term use in HPV- positive women (RR ~1.5 at ≥5y use; effect attenuates post-cessation). HPV is the necessary cofactor. - SUBSTANTIAL PROTECTION against OVARIAN cancer (~50% reduction at ≥5y use, durable for ≥30y post-cessation). - SUBSTANTIAL PROTECTION against ENDOMETRIAL cancer (~50% reduction at ≥5y use, durable post-cessation). - Modest CRC protective association (less robust). Net cancer balance is favorable for most users. Prevention-persona RedFlag triggers a COUNSELING-BALAN...

Notas

Prevenção-persona RedFlag para uso combinado de longo prazo OCP ≥10y. Perfil de câncer misto — aumento de risco modesto (peito, cervical com-HPV) E proteção substancial (ovário, endometrial). Rotas motoras para PreventionPlan recomendando indicação de CONSEGUIMENTO-BALANCE, não alerta de risco unidirecional. Critical que torna a camada apresentar painel de risco-benefício completo.
