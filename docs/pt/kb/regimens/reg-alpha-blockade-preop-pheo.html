Notas

This regimen models the mandatory preoperative alpha-adrenergic blockade step described in the Disease narrative -- NOT a cytotoxic or antineoplastic regimen. It is the recommended_regimen on IND-PHEO-LOCALIZED-1L-ALPHA-BLOCKADE-ADRENALECTOMY and exists purely to prevent intraoperative catecholamine-release hypertensive crisis during adrenalectomy. Adequacy of blockade is gated by RF-PHEO-INADEQUATE-ALPHA-BLOCKADE, a hard hold on proceeding to surgery. No fixed target BP/HR numeric threshold is asserted here -- "adequate" blockade is a clinical judgment per Endocrine Society 2014 guidance, not a KB-invented number.
