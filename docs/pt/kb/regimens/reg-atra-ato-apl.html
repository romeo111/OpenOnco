Notas

Standard 1L for low/intermediate-risk APL (Sanz WBC ≤10). APL0406: 2-y EFS 97% vs 86% with ATRA+chemo (AIDA), OS 99% vs 91%, less hematologic toxicity, no anthracycline cardiotoxicity. ATRA initiated IMMEDIATELY at clinical APL suspicion (do NOT wait for cytogenetic confirmation). Daily ECG + electrolyte protocol mandatory during ATO induction. STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff before publication.
