Evidence for specific statements

These citations cover only the statements and scopes shown. They do not establish clinical approval of this record.

Bleomycin exposure warrants perioperative pulmonary-risk assessment. Label precautions favor lower oxygen concentrations, but observational evidence does not establish a universal FiO2 ceiling. Anesthesia should balance pulmonary risk with adequate oxygenation; a fixed limit must not cause inadequate oxygenation.

Perioperative oxygen management after bleomycin only; no verification of other BEP dosing or supportive-care statements.

Bleomycin label pulmonary adverse reactions; Aakre 2014 Results/Discussion; PMID 6200191 abstract.

Primary G-CSF prophylaxis is not universally required for metastatic testicular BEP; clinicians may choose it to maintain dose intensity. Supportive therapy follows individual assessment and institutional protocol.

G-CSF supportive-care selection only; no approval of anticancer dosing, renal adjustments or treatment eligibility.

SRC-EVIQ-TESTICULAR-BEP-320

eviQ 320 v9 treatment schedule footnote ** and supportive therapy defaults paragraph.

New cough or dyspnea during bleomycin treatment requires assessment for pulmonary toxicity; HRCT is preferred when toxicity is suspected. PFT/DLCO is adjunctive, is not a sole automatic stop rule and does not guarantee prevention of pulmonary toxicity.

Pulmonary symptom assessment and role of PFT/DLCO only; no blanket validation of other BEP claims.

SRC-BLEOMYCIN-UK-BEST-PRACTICE-2018

British Journal of Cancer best-practice guideline: role of PFTs and symptom monitoring sections.

BEP dose delays or reductions considered for kidney dysfunction require review by an experienced testicular-cancer centre because efficacy may be compromised. No universal cisplatin dose-reduction table or automatic switch to EP is established by this record.

Specialist review of kidney-related BEP dosing decisions only; no individualized dose prescription or blanket validation of other dose modifications.

SRC-EVIQ-TESTICULAR-BEP-320

eviQ 320 v9 Dose modifications: note on delays/reductions and renal dysfunction.

In the 1997 MRC/EORTC randomized study of good-risk metastatic nonseminomatous germ-cell cancer, the tested carboplatin-based CEB regimen was inferior to the tested cisplatin-based BEP regimen. This does not establish an alternative regimen for severe kidney impairment.

Comparative efficacy in the trial population and tested schedules only; not all germ-cell tumours, carboplatin indications or patients with severe kidney impairment.
