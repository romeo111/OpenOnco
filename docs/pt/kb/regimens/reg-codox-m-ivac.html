Notas

Scoped reference schedule repair based on eviQ 4489 v1 and 4490 v2, for high-risk Burkitt WITHOUT proven CNS disease. This is not original Magrath or LMB and not a universal Burkitt/CNS prescription. The indication now requires explicit high-risk and CNS-negative confirmation. Risk classification, broader eligibility, ranking and other protocol variants still require separate audits. Age, fitness, efficacy, availability and patient watchpoints are not validated by this repair. Mesna schedules may vary by institution. Intrathecal procedures must remain separate from IV drug administration; vincristine must NEVER be administered intrathecally.
