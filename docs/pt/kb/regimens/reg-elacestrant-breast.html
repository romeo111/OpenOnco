Notas

EMERALD: ESR1-mutant cohort PFS 3.8 vs 1.9 mo (HR 0.55). Benefit enriched in patients with ≥12 mo prior CDK4/6i exposure (post-hoc). ESR1-mutation testing (ctDNA preferred — polyclonal, acquired on AI) required pre-start. Other oral SERDs in late development (camizestrant, giredestrant, imlunestrant); elacestrant is the first FDA-approved (2023).
