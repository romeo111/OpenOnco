Notas

Triplet backbone (irinotecan + oxaliplatin + 5-FU/LV) + bevacizumab. TRIBE: mOS 29.8 vs 25.8 mo vs FOLFIRI+bev (HR 0.80). TRIBE2: PFS2 19.2 vs 16.4 mo (HR 0.74). Substantially more toxic than doublet; requires ECOG 0-1, age ideally <75, normal organ function, no prior pelvic radiation to bowel. G-CSF prophylaxis mandatory. Preferred for: high tumour burden, conversion-intent liver-limited disease, BRAF V600E mCRC (poor prognosis, benefit from aggressive induction). Not appropriate for frail, elderly, or post-bowel resection patients with impaired recovery.
