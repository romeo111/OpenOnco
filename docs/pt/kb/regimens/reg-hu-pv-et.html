Notas

Standard 1L cytoreduction for high-risk PV (age >60 OR prior thrombosis) AND high-risk ET (same criteria). Targets: - PV: Hct <45% (mainstay; CYTO-PV trial), platelets <400K - ET: platelets <400-450K Phlebotomy is the foundation in PV (with HU layered for cytoreduction); ASA at low dose unless contraindicated (extreme thrombocytosis with acquired vWD = relative contraindication). STUB — requires clinical co-lead signoff before publication.
