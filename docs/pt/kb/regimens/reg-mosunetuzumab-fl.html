Notas

GO29781 (Budde 2022): r/r FL after ≥2 prior lines (median 3 prior lines, 69% double-refractory) — ORR 80%, CR 60%, median DOR 22.8 mo, mPFS 17.9 mo. Time-limited (max 17 cycles) vs continuous BTKi. CRS predominantly cycle 1 step-up; Grade ≥3 ~2% — far below CAR-T. Cycle 1 hospitalization (for monitoring after step-up doses) standard; subsequent cycles outpatient. Ambulatory option vs CAR-T (one-shot, complex logistics) and tazemetostat (oral but lower CR rate). Boxed CRS warning, not BBW.
