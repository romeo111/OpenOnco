Notas

Wegovy (semaglutide 2.4 mg weekly) is first-in-class GLP-1 RA approved for chronic obesity management. ~12-15% mean weight loss in STEP trials, sustained ≥68 weeks. Contraindicated in personal/family history of MTC or MEN2. Discontinue ≥2 months before pregnancy. Most weight loss is regained on discontinuation — long-term continuation expected. Obesity is associated with 13+ cancers (IARC/WCRF).
