Notas

O'Donnell et al. (Br J Haematol 2018) — VRd-lite phase II in transplant-ineligible MM (median age 73): ORR ~86%, ≥VGPR ~66%, median PFS 35 mo, with substantially less toxicity than full-dose VRd. Weekly bortezomib SC + reduced lenalidomide (15 mg vs 25 mg) + weekly dexamethasone (20 mg vs 40 mg). 35-day cycle (vs 21-day full VRd) gives recovery time. Reasonable alternative to D-Rd (MAIA) when daratumumab not accessible. Frailty-adapted dosing per IMWG.
