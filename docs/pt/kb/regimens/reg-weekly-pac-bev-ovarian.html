Notas

AURELIA weekly-paclitaxel arm: mPFS 10.4 vs 3.9 mo (HR 0.46); ORR 53.3% vs 30.2%. OS subgroup analysis: 22.4 vs 13.2 mo (HR 0.65) — only AURELIA chemo arm with statistically significant OS benefit. Preferred AURELIA backbone when no contraindication to paclitaxel re-exposure (no significant residual neuropathy).
