Notas

Consulted 2026-10-12: sections 2.4 and 5.3 for primary G-CSF prophylaxis beginning with Cycle 1 in adults with previously untreated PTCL receiving ADCETRIS+CHP. Other indications, dose modifications and regulatory/availability claims not audited. Additional scoped review 2026-10-13 of US section 1.5 through official Pfizer labeling: adult previously untreated CD30-expressing PTCL includes AITL and PTCL-NOS with CHP. Authorization does not establish subgroup superiority, universal CD30 percentage cutoff or Ukrainian/European authorization. Scoped review 2026-10-13: sections 4, 5.1 and adult Table 2 at 1.8 mg/kg every 3 weeks with chemotherapy. Bleomycin combination contraindication and sensory/motor grade-specific neuropathy modifications; no baseline eligibility or whole-label approval. Scoped review 2026-10-13: adult first-line PTCL Table 1, 1.8 mg/kg maximum 180 mg, weight above 100 kg calculated at 100 kg, every 3 weeks for 6–8 doses. No individual course selection or organ-function eligibility approval.
