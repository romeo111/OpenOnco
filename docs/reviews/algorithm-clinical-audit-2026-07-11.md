### A. Safety gating (highest concern — a hazardous sequence/contraindication not enforced)  (3)

**`aml_1l`** — [HIGH] AML emergency (hyperleukocytosis/TLS/leukostasis) is pinned to VEN+AZA, contradicting the regimen's own contraindication flag and venetoclax TLS hazard
  - **Problem:** Step 1 routes RF-AML-EMERGENCY-TLS-LEUKOSTASIS (WBC ≥100K, active TLS, or symptomatic leukostasis) directly to result IND-AML-1L-VEN-AZA as the 'lowest-toxicity standard.' Two problems: (1) IND-AML-1L-VEN-AZA itself lists RF-AML-EMERGENCY-TLS-LEUKOSTASIS in red_flags_triggering_alternative — i.e. the indication says this RF should route AWAY from ven+aza, so the algorithm directly contradicts its own selected indication; (2) venetoclax ramp-up in a high-tumor-burden / hyperleukocytotic state is itself a major TLS driver and per label/practice requires cytoreduction BEFORE venetoclax initiation — so pinning an active-TLS/hyperleukocytosis patient to VEN+AZA is clinically hazardous. Many of these patients are young and fit and, after cytoreduction, belong on intensive 7+3, not ven+aza. The RF's own definition is a treatment HOLD (cytoreduction, rasburicase, leukapheresis), not a signal to pick ven+aza.
  - **Should be:** The emergency RF should trigger a HOLD/stabilization pathway (hydroxyurea ± leukapheresis, hydration + rasburicase/allopurinol, electrolyte correction) and must NOT auto-select venetoclax-based therapy; after stabilization, backbone (7+3 vs VEN+AZA) is chosen by fitness, not pinned to VEN+AZA. Venetoclax should not be initiated during active TLS / uncontrolled hyperleukocytosis.
  - **Basis:** NCCN AML 2025 (hyperleukocytosis/leukostasis — urgent cytoreduction then fitness-based induction); Venetoclax (Venclexta) US label / VIALE-A TLS-mitigation requirements (cytoreduce WBC and ramp with TLS prophylaxis before/at initiation)

**`atll_1l`** — [HIGH · PubMed✓] Frontline mogamulizumab is bundled with planned allo-SCT for all aggressive ATLL, without gating the mogamulizumab→transplant severe-GVHD hazard
  - **Problem:** IND-ATLL-1L-AGGRESSIVE (the aggressive-track output) adds mogamulizumab as concurrent therapy and lists allo-SCT in CR1 as followed_by for every aggressive/transplant-eligible patient. Pre-transplant mogamulizumab is associated with markedly increased severe/steroid-refractory GVHD and transplant-related mortality — the exact hazard the sister algorithm ALGO-ATLL-2L enforces with a mandatory ≥50-day washout and FDA black-box citation. The indication's own do_not_do says 'Do not give mogamulizumab before planned allo-SCT,' yet the algorithm routes the transplant-bound aggressive population straight into that sequence with no washout gate or fork separating transplant candidates.
  - **Should be:** In transplant-eligible aggressive ATLL, avoid (or enforce a long washout after) mogamulizumab before allo-SCT due to severe GVHD/TRM risk; frontline mogamulizumab should be gated on transplant intent, not applied as unconditional concurrent therapy.
  - **Basis:** FDA mogamulizumab (Poteligeo) safety labeling on pre-transplant use; Fuji et al. (JCO 2016) increased GVHD/TRM after pre-alloSCT mogamulizumab; NCCN T-cell/ATLL guidance.
  - **Verifier evidence:** Fuji S, et al. 'Pretransplantation Anti-CCR4 Antibody Mogamulizumab Against Adult T-Cell Leukemia/Lymphoma Is Associated With Significantly Increased Risks of Severe and Corticosteroid-Refractory Graft-Versus-Host Disease, Nonrelapse Mortality, and Overall Mortality.' J Clin Oncol 2016;34(28):3426-3433. PMID 27507878, DOI 10.1200/JCO.2016.67.8250. Grade 3-4 aGVHD RR 1.80 (P<.01); steroid-refractory aGVHD RR 2.09 (P<.01); 1-yr NRM 43.7% vs 25.1% (P<.01); 1-yr OS 32.3% vs 49.4% (P<.01); mogamulizumab <50 days before allo-HSCT = dismal outcome; concludes cautious use in allo-HSCT-eligible ATLL. **`endometrial_2l`** — [HIGH · PubMed✓] Frail / lenvatinib-unfit patients are funneled into pembrolizumab+lenvatinib; the fitness gates are non-functional and frail dMMR patients are denied single-agent IO
  - **Problem:** Step 1 (single-agent dostarlimab/pembro for dMMR, no prior ICI) requires ECOG-fit or ECOG-intermediate. A frail dMMR patient (ECOG ≥3, or failing the fitness RF) drops to step 2, whose both branches return IND-ENDOMETRIAL-2L-PEMBRO-LENVA-PMMR.   - **Basis:** KEYNOTE-158/GARNET (single-agent pembrolizumab/dostarlimab for dMMR/MSI-H) and KEYNOTE-775 (Makker, NEJM 2022, pembro+lenvatinib) — NCCN Uterine 2025 reserves the lenvatinib combo for patients able to tolerate anti-angiogenic toxicity
  - **Verifier evidence:** Algorithm file algo_endometrial_2l.yaml: step 1 (lines 13-29) gates single-agent IO behind ECOG-FIT/INTERMEDIATE; step 2 (lines 30-40) returns IND-ENDOMETRIAL-2L-PEMBRO-LENVA-PMMR on both branches (if_true line 37, if_false line 39); step-2 notes (line 40) and file notes item 4 (lines 57-59) concede the lenvatinib gate does not switch indication and the doxorubicin/paclitaxel fallback is unmodeled.
